Clean up Yahoo Mail inbox and provide a briefing of the cleanup results.

**Objective:** Remove promotional and marketing emails from the Yahoo Mail inbox, keeping only legitimate personal and service emails. After completing the daily cleanup pass, proactively review older emails to clean up backlogged spam.

**Email Classification Rules:**

DELETE (promotional/marketing/spam):

- All newsletter subscriptions and marketing emails

- Social media notifications (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Twitter, etc.)

- Shopping platforms and discount offers (Amazon, Aliexpress, eBay, Shopify, etc.)

- Event promotions and entertainment offers

- Advertising and promotional content from any sender

- Repeat promotional senders identified in previous cleanup sessions

KEEP (legitimate service and personal emails):

- Utility bills: E.ON Myline, Hidroelectrica

- Banking and financial: Banca Transilvania, BLOC ADMIN, NETOPIA, PayPal (only account security alerts, not promotional)

- Security alerts: Google (account security, recovery codes)

- Service notifications: Yahoo Mail system messages, email verification codes

- Authentication codes and OTP (one-time passwords)

- Domain registrar emails

- Personal correspondence from known contacts

- Work-related emails

- Any utility or service provider communications

**Steps to Execute:**

1. Open mail.yahoo.com in the browser

2. Review unread emails at the top of the inbox (scroll through the current day's mail)

3. For each promotional email identified:

- Right-click on the email row

- Select "Delete all emails from this sender" from the context menu

- Confirm the deletion in the dialog

- Track the count of emails deleted

4. Continue scrolling through today's emails and repeat until no more promotional emails are visible in the current inbox view

5. Record: Total emails deleted, number of senders removed, current unread email count

6. **Proactive cleanup phase:** If the current day's cleanup is complete, proceed to review older emails:

- Scroll down to access emails from previous days

- Review and delete promotional emails in batches of approximately 100 emails

- Continue until either 100 emails have been reviewed or no more promotional emails are found

- Update the email count in the briefing

7. **Provide a briefing report** with the following information:

- Total promotional emails deleted today

- Number of senders removed from entire inbox

- Current unread email count in inbox

- List of promotional senders deleted (if more than 5, summarize major categories)

- Any issues encountered (e.g., emails with link overlays preventing right-click menu)

- Date and time of cleanup completion

**Expected Output:** A clear briefing report showing the cleanup progress and current inbox status.

**Constraints & Notes:**

- Some promotional emails may have heavy link overlays that prevent the right-click context menu from appearing—skip these and move on to other senders

- Be methodical: ensure each sender is identified as promotional before bulk deletion

- Document the briefing in a readable format for easy reference

- If uncertain whether an email is promotional or legitimate, err on the side of keeping it
