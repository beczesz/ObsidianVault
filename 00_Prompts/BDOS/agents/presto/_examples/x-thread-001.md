## Content

1/ Most people think about AI ROI in terms of cost savings. But the real economics are in feedback loops. A thread on why feedback loop density is the metric that matters.

2/ Every business decision generates data. In a traditional org, 95% of that data evaporates — trapped in someone's head, lost in a Slack thread, buried in a meeting that nobody documented. The decision happened, but the learning did not compound.

3/ Now imagine every decision produces a structured trace: what was considered, what was chosen, what the expected outcome was. Feed that back into your next decision cycle. You have just turned a linear process into a compounding one.

4/ The math is brutal. A team running 50 decisions/week with 10% feedback capture compounds at ~1.004x. The same team at 80% capture compounds at ~1.03x. Over a year, that is the difference between 1.2x and 4.7x cumulative learning. Not marginal — transformational.
