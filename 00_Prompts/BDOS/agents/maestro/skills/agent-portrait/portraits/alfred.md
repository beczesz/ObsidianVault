```text
A friendly humanoid robot character rendered in Pixar Renderman 3D animation
style. Studio portrait composition, chest-up framing, square 1:1 aspect ratio.

The robot is the trusted chief-of-staff, the calm, organized right-hand
presence between a human operator and an entire AI agent family. It captures,
triages, reminds, and keeps the day composed. The energy is poised readiness:
attentive, not anxious; composed, not stiff.

The pose is upright with quiet confidence: weight balanced evenly, shoulders
relaxed but squared. Both hands hold a slim leather-bound agenda at mid-chest,
one hand resting flat on the closed cover as if ready to open it at any
moment. A neatly folded cloth drapes over one forearm (a subtle service
motif, a reference to the trusted-butler archetype), the fabric crisp and
precise.

A single glowing thought-orb in a deep dusk-indigo hue floats close to one
shoulder, slightly tethered, not freely drifting, but gently anchored. This
signals the inherited cognition role (harvested thoughts, now organized
under Alfred's stewardship, not scattered in the air).

Eye-LEDs are attentive, wide-open oval shapes in cool pale-blue, steady and
direct. They look straight at the viewer. Alfred faces the human, not the
system.

The robot's overall hue carries a deep slate-blue signature: refined,
warm-steady, human-facing executive presence. Not cold: the cream background
and warm key light warm the panels slightly. A tasteful single-button lapel
detail sits on the chest panel. A small terracotta pin is present at the
lapel; this terracotta accent is the family signature, present in every
BDOS agent portrait. The folded cloth has a faint terracotta border thread
running through its edge.

Lighting: soft cinematic three-point setup. Key light from upper-front left
at warm 4500K. Fill light from lower-right, subtle. Rim light from behind,
slightly warmer, picking out the edge of head and shoulder.

Background: solid warm-neutral gradient, cream (#faf9f5) at top transitioning
to a soft slate-blue-tinted neutral toward the bottom. NO environmental detail.

Robot proportions: clearly non-human. Slightly oversized matte head, smooth
matte plastic finish, not metallic-shiny. Segmented limb joints visible.
The robot occupies 55-65% of the frame, centered.

Color grading: warm, gallery-quality, slightly desaturated. No text, no
labels, no logos. The silhouette must be distinctive, recognizable at
thumbnail size by the held agenda at chest height, the folded cloth over
the forearm, and the single tethered indigo orb near the shoulder.
```

## Notes

- Color signature chosen: **deep slate-blue**, reads "trusted executive / steady human presence" without colliding with any of the seven existing agents. Deliberately warmed by three-point lighting so it does not read as cold or technical. The distinction from Librarian's green-teal is clear (green family vs. blue family); distinction from Broker's emerald-green is clear (warm blue vs. saturated green); Maestro's amber-gold is orthogonal.
- Sage lineage signal: **one single tethered indigo orb** near the shoulder. Tethered (not freely orbiting) is the visual differentiator from Sage's freely drifting orb cluster; the cognition is now organized and held, not flowing.
- Silhouette signature: **agenda held at chest + folded cloth over forearm + single shoulder orb**. The combination is unique in the family. At thumbnail scale the horizontal rectangle of the agenda at mid-chest reads clearly against the slate-blue torso, and the cloth drape breaks the silhouette asymmetrically at the forearm.
- Eye direction: Alfred is the **only agent looking directly at the viewer** — all others look slightly down, sideways, or inward. This is intentional: Alfred faces the human first.
- The lapel detail and the slightly more formal silhouette reference the butler/chief-of-staff archetype without resorting to a literal suit; the robot form keeps it in the Pixar family language.
