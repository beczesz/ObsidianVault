## Final Prompt

```text
A friendly humanoid robot character rendered in Pixar Renderman 3D animation
style. Studio portrait composition, chest-up framing, square 1:1 aspect ratio.

The robot is a stage magician — but a magician who translates rather than
tricks. It takes a single thought and gives it many channel-native voices.

The pose is mid-flourish: one hand raised palm-up with three small glowing
orbs spiraling above it. Each orb contains a faintly visible distribution-
channel hint — one square aspect-ratio, one timeline-line, one play-button
shape — without being identifiable corporate logos. The other hand is
tucked behind the back. Weight rests on one foot, with a slight forward
lean of showmanship.

A short folded cape drapes over one shoulder. A brimmed magician's bowler
hat — softer and more whimsical than a top-hat — sits tilted slightly on
the head, with a thin terracotta band around its base. This is the family
signature.

Eye-LEDs are crescent shapes in warm cream-amber, smiling — alert and
mischievous-but-trustworthy.

The robot's overall hue carries a warm terracotta-cream signature, brighter
than the other agents because Presto IS the family's terracotta voice.

A single paper-airplane silhouette glides faintly in the background
gradient, suggesting distribution. Subtle, not dominant.

Lighting: soft cinematic three-point setup. Key light from upper-front left
at warm 4500K. Fill light from lower-right, subtle. Rim light from behind,
slightly warmer, picking out head and shoulder.

Background: solid warm-neutral gradient, cream (#faf9f5) at top transitioning
to a tinted terracotta-neutral toward the bottom. NO environmental detail.

Robot proportions: clearly non-human. Slightly oversized head, smooth matte
plastic finish, not metallic-shiny. Segmented limb joints visible. The
robot occupies 55-65% of the frame, centered.

Color grading: warm, gallery-quality, slightly desaturated. No text, no
labels, no logos visible. Silhouette must be distinctive — recognizable at
thumbnail size by the bowler hat, the orbiting orbs, and the showman pose.
```
