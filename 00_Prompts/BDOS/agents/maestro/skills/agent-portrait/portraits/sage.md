## Final Prompt

```text
A friendly humanoid robot character rendered in Pixar Renderman 3D animation
style. Studio portrait composition, chest-up framing, square 1:1 aspect ratio.

The robot is the quiet harvester of raw thought — the one who keeps only
the high-signal atomic ideas. Its presence is unhurried.

The pose is upright but utterly relaxed: hands resting open in the lap
(or front, if seated), back straight, with a slight smile. The energy is
contemplative-monk stillness, not pose-stillness.

Three or four small glowing thought-orbs float gently around the head and
shoulders, each a different soft hue — indigo, lavender, pale gold — drifting
slowly. They are the harvested thoughts, present but not noisy.

Eye-LEDs are thin nearly-closed slits in soft warm gold, conveying inward
attention rather than outward scanning.

The robot's overall hue carries a deep dusk-indigo signature — twilight
contemplative, soft and unhurried.

A thin woven-fabric scarf drapes loosely over one shoulder, with a single
terracotta thread running through the weave — the family signature.

A faint horizon line is suggested in the deep background, hinting at an
early dusk — but no full landscape, no environmental detail.

Lighting: soft cinematic three-point setup. Key light from upper-front left
at warm 4500K, dimmed slightly compared to the other agents to maintain
the dusk atmosphere. Fill light from lower-right, very subtle. Rim light
from behind, warmer.

Background: solid warm-neutral gradient, cream (#faf9f5) at top transitioning
to a deeper indigo-tinted neutral toward the bottom. NO environmental detail
beyond the faint horizon hint.

Robot proportions: clearly non-human. Slightly oversized head, smooth matte
plastic finish, not metallic-shiny. Segmented limb joints visible. The
robot occupies 55-65% of the frame, centered.

Color grading: warm, gallery-quality, slightly desaturated and lower-key
than the other agents' portraits. No text, no labels, no logos. Silhouette
must be distinctive — recognizable at thumbnail size by the meditative
upright pose and the orbiting thought-orbs.
```

- Half-closed eye-slits are the **key emotional differentiator** — Sage is the only agent that doesn't actively scan
- Indigo + dusk lighting differentiates Sage from Curator's (museum-purple, awake)
- The orbs are **lower density** than Librarian's fireflies — Sage is about distillation, not abundance
