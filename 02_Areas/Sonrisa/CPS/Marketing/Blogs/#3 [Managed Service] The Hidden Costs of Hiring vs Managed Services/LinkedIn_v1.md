You budget EUR 70,000 for a DevOps engineer.

By the end of year one, you have spent EUR 150,000. Sometimes more.

Recruitment agency fee, employer taxes, three months before they are actually productive, tooling licenses, training budget, management time. None of these are unusual costs. None of them are in the original budget.

And that is before you account for what happens if they leave after 18 months - which, statistically, is quite likely. Then you do it all again.

I am not writing this to push anyone toward a managed service. I am writing it because I have watched too many finance and engineering leaders make this decision based on the offer letter number, and then discover the real number a year later.

If you are weighing the options right now, at least start with the full picture. I broke down the real year-one cost of a senior DevOps hire line by line, and compared it directly to what a managed service actually costs, including every add-on.
