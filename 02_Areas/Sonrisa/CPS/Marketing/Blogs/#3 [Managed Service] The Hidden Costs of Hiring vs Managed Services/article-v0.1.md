## The True Cost of Hiring One DevOps Engineer

The table below breaks down what a single senior DevOps hire actually costs in year one. These figures are based on EU market rates.

| Cost Category | Estimated Annual Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Base salary | EUR 60,000-80,000 | Senior level, EU market |
| Employer taxes and benefits | EUR 15,000-25,000 | Approximately 25-35% on top of salary |
| Recruitment cost | EUR 10,000-20,000 | Agency fees or internal HR time |
| Onboarding and ramp-up | EUR 5,000-10,000 | 2-3 months to full productivity |
| Tooling and licenses | EUR 3,000-8,000 | Monitoring, CI/CD, security tools |
| Training and conferences | EUR 2,000-5,000 | Certifications, skill development |
| Management overhead | EUR 5,000-10,000 | 1:1s, performance reviews, team integration |
| **Total Year 1** | **EUR 100,000-158,000** | **vs. EUR 70,000 budgeted salary** |

Two things make this worse over time.

**The turnover multiplier.** Average DevOps tenure is roughly two years. That means you pay this ramp-up cost every 24 months - recruitment, onboarding, knowledge transfer, all over again.

**The vacancy gap.** Average time to hire a senior DevOps engineer: 3-6 months. What happens to your infrastructure during that gap? Who handles the incidents, the deployments, the security patches?

## The Cost Nobody Calculates - Downtime and Incidents

Beyond salary and overhead, there is a cost most companies only recognise after the fact: what happens when things go wrong.

Cost of downtime for mid-sized companies ranges from EUR 5,000 to EUR 50,000 per hour depending on business type. A single major incident can cost more than an entire year of managed service.

The math is simple: **the cost of prevention is always lower than the cost of recovery.**

Two real scenarios we see regularly:

**Scenario 1: The surprise cloud bill.** A misconfigured auto-scaling rule leads to a EUR 15,000 cloud bill in a single month. A FinOps review (EUR 500/month add-on) would have caught it within the first week.

**Scenario 2: The weekend outage.** A 4-hour outage during business hours costs EUR 20,000 in lost revenue. 24/7 on-call support (EUR 2,000/month) reduces mean time to resolution from hours to minutes.

In both cases, the preventive cost is a fraction of the damage. Proactive management is not an expense. It is insurance that pays for itself.

## Side-by-Side - When Each Option Wins

| Factor | In-House Hire | Managed Service |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 cost | EUR 100,000-158,000 | EUR 24,000-72,000 |
| Time to full capability | 3-6 months | 2-4 weeks |
| Breadth of expertise | 2-3 specialties | Full team coverage |
| 24/7 availability | No (without 3+ hires) | Yes (add-on) |
| Scalability | Fixed capacity | Flexible hours |
| Knowledge retention | Single point of failure | Team-documented |
| Best when | DevOps is core, 500+ team | 5-500 employees, growing fast |

This is not about one option being universally better. It is about which option fits your company's stage, budget, and risk tolerance.

Many of our clients start with a managed service and add an internal hire as they grow. The hybrid path - one internal engineer plus managed service coverage - is often the most cost-effective model for companies in the 50-500 employee range.
