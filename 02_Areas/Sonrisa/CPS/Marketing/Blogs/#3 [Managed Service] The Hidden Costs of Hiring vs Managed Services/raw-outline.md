### Section 1: The True Cost of Hiring One DevOps Engineer (350 words)

**Key points — the iceberg breakdown:**

| Cost Category | Estimated Annual Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Base salary | €60,000-€80,000 | Senior level, EU market |
| Employer taxes & benefits | €15,000-€25,000 | ~25-35% on top of salary |
| Recruitment cost | €10,000-€20,000 | Agency fees or internal HR time |
| Onboarding & ramp-up | €5,000-€10,000 | 2-3 months to full productivity |
| Tooling & licenses | €3,000-€8,000 | Monitoring, CI/CD, security tools |
| Training & conferences | €2,000-€5,000 | Certifications, skill development |
| Management overhead | €5,000-€10,000 | 1:1s, performance reviews, team integration |
| **Total Year 1** | **€100,000-€158,000** | **vs. €70k budgeted salary** |

- **The turnover multiplier:** Average DevOps tenure ~2 years. So you pay this ramp-up cost every 24 months.
- **The vacancy gap:** Average time to hire senior DevOps: 3-6 months. What happens to your infrastructure during that gap?
- **BMC Connection — Customer Pain Points:** "We keep spending on hiring but never feel fully covered"

### Section 2: The Cost Nobody Calculates — Downtime and Incidents (250 words)

**Key points:**
- Cost of downtime for mid-sized companies: €5,000-€50,000 per hour depending on business type
- A single major incident can cost more than an entire year of managed service
- **The prevention equation:** Proactive management cost vs. reactive incident cost
- **BMC Connection — Key Activities:**
  - Proactive monitoring & alerting (catches issues before they become outages)
  - Incident response with defined SLAs
  - Infrastructure-as-code preventing configuration drift
- **CPS Value — Proaktivitás:** "Megelőzés, nem reagálás" (Prevention, not reaction) — the cost of prevention is always lower than the cost of recovery

**Supporting data/examples:**
- Example scenario: a misconfigured auto-scaling rule leads to a €15,000 cloud bill surprise. A FinOps review (€500/month add-on) would have caught it.
- Example: 4-hour outage during business hours = €20,000 in lost revenue. 24/7 on-call (€2,000/month) reduces mean time to resolution from hours to minutes.
