# CPS Blog Series - Competitive Brief
## Research Date: March 25, 2026

## Context
This brief was created to inform the CPS blog series targeting IT leaders in "hiring mode." Goal: make them question whether hiring is the right solution, position CPS as the alternative.

## Key Competitors Analyzed

### 1. Palark (palark.com)
- DevOps-as-a-Service + SRE, heavy Kubernetes focus
- Pricing: EUR 3,000-14,000/month (5 tiers, all include dedicated tech lead + PM + 6-8 person team)
- Content: Active technical blog (blog.palark.com) - engineer audience only, no business-decision content
- Threat: Strong technical SEO. If they expand to business content, they rank fast.

### 2. SDH Global (sdh.global)
- DevOps + full software development, Ukraine/Germany/USA
- Pricing: 3 fixed packages (not public, estimated $2K-$6K/month range)
- Content: Already writing about hire vs. outsource - but generic SEO filler quality
- Threat: First-mover SEO advantage on some "hire vs outsource" keywords

### 3. OpsWorks Co. (opsworks.co)
- DevOps and Cloud consulting, AWS Advanced Partner, ISO 27001
- Pricing: Hourly $50-99/hr, project-based ($10K+ min), no subscription model
- Content: Thin blog, case-study focused
- Threat: Low - different model (hourly vs subscription)

## Key Finding
**The "hire vs. outsource" content lane is wide open.** Nobody is doing it well with real credibility + honest tone + IT leader targeting.

## CPS Advantages to Leverage in Content
1. Free entry point (AWS Health Check) - unique, no competitor offers this
2. Transparent tiered pricing (EUR 990-6,000) - lower than Palark, more transparent than SDH/OpsWorks
3. Personal voice (Szabolcs as the face) - competitors are corporate
4. Honest framing ("we'll tell you if in-house is better") - embodies Alazat value

## Recommended Blog Actions
1. Publish Article 1 (Single Engineer Risk) first - targets unowned keywords
2. Include real pricing in Article 2 (Hidden Costs) - transparency as weapon
3. Run SEO audit on target keywords before publishing
4. Write standalone LinkedIn versions with human voice
5. Consider bonus article: "How to evaluate a managed DevOps partner"

## Target Keywords (Low Competition)
- "single devops engineer risk"
- "devops hiring cost"
- "hire devops or outsource"
- "infrastructure bus factor"
