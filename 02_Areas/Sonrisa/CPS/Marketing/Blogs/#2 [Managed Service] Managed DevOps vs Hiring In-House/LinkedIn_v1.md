Most companies never actually decide between hiring in-house and using a managed DevOps service.

They drift into one or the other. A deployment breaks, someone gets hired. A vendor gets called because nobody else is available. Six months later leadership realises the infrastructure model was never a conscious choice - it just happened.

I have had this conversation with probably 50+ companies at this point. And the ones who end up in the worst situations are almost always the ones who never made a deliberate decision. They were just hoping the current setup would hold.

The honest truth: neither in-house nor managed is universally better. They are different bets. The right answer depends on your company stage, how fast you need coverage, and whether DevOps is your core product or just the foundation it runs on.

I put together five questions that actually help make this call. The answers usually point clearly in one direction - and sometimes the answer is both, in the right proportion.
