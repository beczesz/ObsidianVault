Security & Compliance

 

🇪🇺 EU Data Sovereignty

All processing within your designated AWS region or on-prem. No data transfer outside your control.

🔒 Encryption Everywhere

TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest. API key management with per-user quotas and RBAC.
