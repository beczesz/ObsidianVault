The Overbuilt Setup
Paying for infrastructure nobody asked for.
  • Resources provisioned "just in case" that have been running unused for months.
     
  • Multiple environments with no clear ownership, no cleanup schedule, and growing bills.
     
  • The cloud bill keeps climbing, but nobody can explain exactly why.
The One-Person Risk
Your entire infrastructure depends on a single engineer.
  • One person handles deployments, monitoring, security, and firefighting. All of it.
     
  • When they go on vacation, things slow down. When they leave, things break.
     
  • No documentation, no backup plan, and a growing list of "we should really fix that" items.
The Reactive Cycle
Your team only touches infrastructure when something breaks.
  • No proactive monitoring, no regular patching, no cost reviews. Just incident response.
     
  • Outages happen, everyone scrambles, things get patched temporarily, and the cycle repeats.
     
  • Developers spend hours debugging deployment issues instead of shipping features.
The Hiring Dead End
You need DevOps talent, but hiring takes forever.
  • Good DevOps engineers are hard to find, expensive to hire, and take months to onboard.
     
  • By the time you fill the role, the problems have compounded and priorities have shifted.
     
  • And if that hire doesn't work out, you're back to square one with months of lost time.
