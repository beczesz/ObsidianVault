The Challenge

Your team uses AI daily.
But at what cost — and what risk?

Organizations increasingly depend on generative AI, but public LLM services create real barriers for engineering and leadership teams.

🔓

Data Privacy & Compliance Risk

Sending proprietary code, business documents, and sensitive data to external APIs risks breaches and violates internal policies. Critical for regulated industries and EU companies subject to GDPR.

📈

Unpredictable & Escalating Costs

Per-token pricing from OpenAI and Azure scales unpredictably. Enterprises face 20-30% higher costs than self-hosted alternatives, with no cost ceiling and potential for runaway expenses.

🔗

Vendor Lock-In

Limited ability to fine-tune models on your data. No control over model updates or deprecation. Strategic dependency on a single provider's decisions and pricing changes.

⏱️

Performance Constraints

Shared infrastructure means variable latency and throughput limitations under heavy load — problematic for real-time coding assistance or high-volume document processing.

The Solution

Sonrisa LLMaaS Platform

We abstract the complexity of GPU infrastructure, model deployment, and operations into a simple, metered service. Your data never leaves controlled infrastructure.

✓

100% EU Data Residency

All processing within your designated AWS region. No external calls. Full GDPR compliance.

✓

RAG & Fine-Tuning

Retrieval-augmented generation with your documents. LoRA/QLoRA support for domain adaptation.
