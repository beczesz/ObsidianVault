## 4. Go-to-Market Strategy 2026

### Strategic Framework: Wedge-to-Subscription Model

Our 2026 GTM strategy centers on using **low-commitment entry services** as a wedge to build trust, demonstrate value, and convert customers into long-term managed service subscribers. This approach aligns with AWS Marketplace goals for recurring revenue and customer modernization.

#### The Conversion Funnel

1. **Awareness:** Customer discovers CPS via AWS Marketplace organic search, AWS referrals, or marketing campaigns
2. **Entry (Wedge):** Customer requests Free AWS Cost Optimization Assessment ($0 Marketplace listing)
3. **Qualification:** CPS validates customer meets criteria ($1,000+ monthly AWS spend or production workload)
4. **Assessment Delivery:** PDF report + live walkthrough showing potential savings and improvement opportunities
5. **Conversion:** Customer engages for paid optimization project, migration, or managed service subscription
6. **Expansion:** Successful engagement leads to package upgrade or add-on adoption

### Primary Entry Wedge: Free AWS Cost Optimization Assessment

The Free Cost Optimization Assessment is our primary lead generation mechanism. Listed on AWS Marketplace as a **$0 Professional Services offer**, it provides immediate value while showcasing CPS expertise.

#### Assessment Process

1. **Access Provisioning:** Customer grants read-only cross-account IAM role with minimal permissions (Cost Explorer, CloudWatch, billing data, describe-only inventory)
2. **Data Collection:** CPS analyzes spend patterns, resource utilization, idle workloads, and optimization opportunities
3. **Analysis:** Semi-automated process using internal tooling + senior engineer validation
4. **Delivery:** PDF report + 30–60 minute live walkthrough session

#### Report Contents

- Executive summary with key findings
- Detailed breakdown by AWS service category
- Right-sizing recommendations
- Idle and orphaned resource identification
- Reserved Instance / Savings Plan optimization
- Estimated monthly and yearly savings
- Modernization opportunities

#### Qualification Criteria

To ensure engineering resources are focused on qualified prospects:

- **Minimum AWS spend:** $1,000–$2,000 per month
- **OR** at least one active production workload on AWS

### Secondary Wedge: Migration Services

Cloud Migration Services serve as a secondary entry point, particularly for companies transitioning from on-premises or legacy platforms. These projects (€15,000–€100,000) establish deep trust relationships that naturally lead to ongoing managed services.

### Organic Discovery Pathways

1. **AWS Marketplace Search:** Optimized listings for keywords: "AWS managed services," "DevOps support," "cloud cost optimization," "AWS migration"
2. **AWS Partner Referrals:** Direct referrals from AWS account teams seeking managed service partners for customers
3. **AWS Events:** Presence at AWS re:Invent, AWS Summits (EMEA focus), and regional partner events
4. **Content Marketing:** Case studies, whitepapers, and technical blog posts demonstrating CPS expertise

### Content Strategy

1. **Case Studies:** "How CPS Saved €50K Annually for [Customer]" with metrics
2. **Technical Guides:** "AWS Cost Optimization Checklist," "Migration Readiness Assessment Guide"
3. **Webinars:** Quarterly sessions on FinOps, migrations, and DevOps best practices
4. **Blog Content:** SEO-optimized articles targeting technical decision-makers
