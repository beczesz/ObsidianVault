### What It Is

A **Solution** is your company's service offering registered in AWS Partner Central. It appears in the **AWS Partner Solutions Finder** — the searchable directory AWS customers and AWS sales teams use to find qualified partners for specific needs.

Each Solution has its own profile page containing your service description, target industries, AWS service specializations, case studies, certifications, and contact information.

Note: A solution requires a FTR (Foundational Technical Review)

---

### Why It Matters for CPS

**Visibility to AWS Sales Teams:** When an AWS account manager has a customer needing managed DevOps or cost optimization, they search Partner Solutions Finder first. No Solution = invisible to internal AWS.

**Visibility to Customers:** Enterprises researching AWS partners often start here. It's an AWS-endorsed directory, which carries more trust than a Google search result.

**Foundation for Everything Else:** Your Solution is the anchor point. Marketplace listings, case studies, competencies, and Service Delivery Designations all attach to it. Without a well-built Solution, the rest lacks context.

**Supports Your 2026 Targets:** Your strategy relies on "AWS referrals" and "Marketplace organic visitors." Both depend on a discoverable, compelling Solution profile.
