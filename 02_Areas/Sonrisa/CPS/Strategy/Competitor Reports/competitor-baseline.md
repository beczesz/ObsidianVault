## 3. Dedicatted

**URL:** https://dedicatted.com/what-we-do/devops/devops-as-a-service
**HQ:** Not confirmed
**AWS Level:** "Top 2% of AWS consulting partners" (confirmed 2026-03-16); AWS, Azure, GCP certified
**Last checked:** 2026-03-30

### Services
- DevOps as a Service
- 24/7 Platform Support
- DevOps Consulting
- Cloud & AI services
- **Agentic AI** (new as of 2026-03-24; explicit service page at /services/artificial-intelligence/agentic-ai)
- Generative AI Solutions and Consulting

### Pricing
- Subscription-based; starts at 160 hours/month (no smaller packages)
- 14-day exit terms
- No fixed public tier prices — custom quote model
- Covers ~30 production apps at base level; flexible scaling
- Compliance built-in: SOC-2, PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, ISO 27001
- Projects range $15,000–$900,000; hourly rate $50–99/hr (Clutch)

### Recent Content (as of 2026-03-30)
- Case study: **Cassen Testing Laboratories** (Toronto, Canada) -- among Canada's first agentic AI deployments in environmental testing; multi-agent AI platform using Amazon Nova models; ISO 17025 regulated; 3x analytical capacity expansion
- Insights: "Agentic AI Development: Be-All and End-All Leader's Guide"
- Insights: "GenAI vs Agentic AI vs AI Agents" (comparison piece)
- Case studies (prior): FinOps strategy 2026, HIPAA-compliant Azure migration, IoT data architecture with AWS AFT, SOC 2 compliance

### Notes
- On AWS Marketplace as "DevOps as a Service" and "24/7 Platform Support Service"
- Compliance-first positioning is a differentiator vs CPS current messaging
- 14-day exit clause is a notable sales tool (reduces commitment risk for prospects)
- **AI pivot gaining traction** -- real regulated-industry case studies now published (environmental, healthcare, finance)

### Services

- SRE as a Service (SREaaS)

- SOS DevOps Emergency Help

- Flat monthly subscription; size-based (XS to XL)
- GoodFirms recognition: "Best Company to Work With" 2025

### Recent Content
- Case study: "How to migrate to Kubernetes and pay twice less? The case of Adapty" (50% cost reduction)
- Blog: "Kubernetes 1.35: Deep dive into new alpha features"

- Case study: ComplAi compliance SaaS (CI/CD & infrastructure scaling)

### Notes
- "SOS DevOps help!" concept -- interesting emergency service
- Size-based naming (XS-XL) instead of tier names -- simpler UX
