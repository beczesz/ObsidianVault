## Changes Detected

### 1. Kloia — AWS PREMIER Partner (CRITICAL)
**What changed:** AWS partnership level confirmed and upgraded from "Not confirmed" to AWS Premier Tier Services Partner
**Previous:** Not confirmed (baseline noted "NEEDS VERIFICATION")
**Current:** AWS Premier Tier Services Partner — with AWS DevOps Competency and AWS Migration & Modernization Competency
**Strategic implication for CPS:** Kloia operates at the highest possible AWS partner tier (Premier > Advanced > Select), placing them significantly above CPS's current Select tier and above Romexsoft's Advanced tier; this is the strongest competitive signal of the week and reinforces urgency of CPS's Advanced tier upgrade path.

---

### 2. SDH Global — Pricing Confirmed + Active Promotion
**What changed:** Pricing tiers confirmed for first time (previously "NEEDS VERIFICATION"), plus an active limited-time discount is running
**Previous:** "Very similar to CPS pricing" — exact tiers unconfirmed
**Current:** Three tiers at **$2,000 / $4,000 / $6,000 per month** (USD). Active promotion: **20–50% off all one-time implementation tasks** (infrastructure setup, cloud migration, CI/CD, security) when client signs a 1-year managed services contract
**Strategic implication for CPS:** SDH Global pricing is nearly identical to CPS in USD terms (CPS is in EUR, so CPS may actually be priced higher in USD terms at current rates); the 20–50% one-time discount promotion is an aggressive acquisition tactic CPS should monitor and consider countering with a similar onboarding incentive.

---

### 3. Palark — Pricing Confirmed + New Case Study
**What changed:** Price floor confirmed (previously "NEEDS VERIFICATION"), plus a notable Kubernetes cost-reduction case study published
**Previous:** Size-based pricing (XS–XL), exact prices unconfirmed
**Current:** DevOpsaaS from **€3,000/month**; SREaaS from **€9,100/month** (subscription-based, flat monthly)
**New case study:** "How to migrate to Kubernetes and pay twice less? The case of Adapty" — client achieved 50% cost reduction through Kubernetes migration
**Strategic implication for CPS:** Palark's €3,000 entry point is above CPS's Essential (€2,000) and Growth (€4,000) sits in range; the Adapty cost-reduction case study is strong marketing content in CPS's target verticals — CPS should accelerate its own client success story production.

---

### 4. Dedicatted — AWS "Top 2%" Designation Confirmed
**What changed:** AWS partner positioning more precisely defined
**Previous:** AWS level "Not confirmed"
**Current:** Recognized among the **"top 2% of AWS consulting partners"** — 14-day exit terms on subscriptions, 160+ hours/month starting point, SOC-2/PCI DSS/HIPAA/GDPR/ISO 27001 compliance built-in
**Strategic implication for CPS:** Dedicatted's compliance-first positioning (SOC-2, HIPAA, PCI DSS) and flexible exit terms are differentiators CPS does not currently market prominently — worth considering whether to add similar compliance messaging.
