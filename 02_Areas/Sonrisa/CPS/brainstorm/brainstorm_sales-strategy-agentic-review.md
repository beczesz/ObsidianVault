## Key Insights

### UNANIMOUS VERDICT: Execution failure, not strategy failure

All 3 responding AIs (Gemini, Claude Chat, Perplexity) independently reached the same conclusion: **the strategy is sound but we've built an intelligence machine while forgetting to actually sell.**

### Gemini (Validator) -- "Expensive Procrastination"
- "You've built a world-class laboratory to study thirst, but you haven't taken a single sip of water"
- The agentic pipeline is "Infrastructure Porn" -- feels like work but avoids rejection risk
- 15-point matrix is "Analysis Paralysis" -- a security blanket to avoid picking up the phone
- 4-AI stack ROI is "currently negative infinity" -- over-engineering the top of funnel while bottom is a brick wall
- "If you had called those 7 companies on Day 5, you would likely have two POs signed by now"
- ADVICE: "Shut down the pipeline for 48 hours. Take your 7 leads. Have a human send an email or make a call."

### Claude Chat (Domain Expert) -- "Ferrari engine attached to a parked car"
- Funnel benchmarks: 100 cold touches -> 8-12 replies -> 3-5 discoveries -> 2-3 qualified -> 1-2 proposals -> 0.3-0.7 wins
- Need ~150-200 well-targeted first touches per signed deal in year one
- Keep the lead scanner + stack reverse-engineering (genuine differentiator). DROP the multi-AI validation loop for now.
- Free Cloud Health Check is "defensible but not optimal" -- consider paid assessment (EUR 500-1,500), specific diagnostic, or workshop format
- "The first 50 outreach attempts aren't about closing -- they're about discovering your actual reply rate and objection patterns"
- ADVICE: "Send 5 tomorrow. 5 more Wednesday. All 15 by Friday. Pause multi-AI validation for 2 weeks."
- Weekly metric: first touches sent/week, target 25/week by Day 45

### Perplexity (Researcher) -- Sourced benchmarks
- Cold outreach to meeting: 1-5% email, 5-8% phone (managed IT services)
- 8-12 touch sequences across channels to get first meeting
- 20-40 total touches before close in complex B2B
- Proposal to close: median 25%, top quartile 35%+
- Sales cycle 30-90 days for EUR 1-6K/mo deals
- Mission Cloud, DoIT, Rackspace all use partner-led (AWS marketplace) + consultative process
- CEE-specific: trust and local references carry DISPROPORTIONATE weight
- CAC for managed cloud in 1-6K/mo range: benchmarked but varies
- ADVICE: Tighten ICP to 1-2 verticals where you have inherited clients, productize 2-3 specific offers, start with lighthouse accounts
