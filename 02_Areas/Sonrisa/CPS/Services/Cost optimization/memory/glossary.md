## Service & Product Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

## Technical & Industry Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| FinOps | Cloud financial operations — discipline of cloud cost governance |
| CUR | Cost & Usage Report (AWS billing data) |
| Rightsizing | Adjusting instance sizes to match actual workload needs |
| Commitment coverage | Reserved Instances or Savings Plans aligned to steady workloads |
| Tiering | Distributing storage across cost tiers (Standard, IA, Glacier) |

## Strategic Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Strategic wedge | The Health Check's role — low-friction entry leading to CPS engagements |
| Signal extraction | Core purpose of Health Check — surface cost signals, not solve problems |
| Disqualification | Intentional feature — cleanly exit poor-fit clients with minimal cost |
| Self-funding narrative | Pattern (not promise) that CPS savings can offset CPS fees |
