# **Engagement Model**

### **1. Discovery and Assessment**

We start with a Cloud Maturity Review assessing your AWS architecture, security posture, operational readiness, and cost profile. The result is a clear, actionable roadmap highlighting risks, quick wins, and modernization opportunities.

### **2. Onboarding and Implementation**

A dedicated engineering team (Senior, Expert, Junior) led by a Technical Account Manager establishes operational baselines, sets up monitoring and observability, implements automation, reviews security configurations, and executes prioritized improvements such as right-sizing, CI/CD optimization, or reliability enhancements.

### **3. Ongoing Operations and Optimization**

Depending on your package (40, 80, or 120 hours/month), we deliver continuous monitoring, incident response with SLAs starting at four hours, environment updates, CI/CD changes, infrastructure automation, and monthly FinOps reviews delivering typical savings of 10–30%. Quarterly reviews ensure strategic alignment with your roadmap.
