### How the "$5,000 enabler" actually works (Partner Help guide, a_id 1020948, modified 2026-02-27)

**Correction to earlier understanding:** the "$5,000 enabler" is NOT a free credit grant. Under Enhanced OPN (numerical Levels), the Level 1+ benefit is the **right to purchase Oracle Universal Credits (UC) at a 55% discount** off rate card, for development, testing, and demonstration. The old "$5,000 free enabler" framing came from the previous (Track-based) OPN program; the $5,000 figure now appears as a minimum UC order when paying with Partner Credits.

Key facts:
- **Discount:** 55% off rate card on UC for dev/test/demo. Payable with cash OR Oracle Partner Credits (we have 0 partner credits).
- **Services period:** 12 months from UC purchase date (does not align with Level subscription end date).
- **Usage scope:** any OCI IaaS/PaaS, any region. For training, integration testing, solution demonstrations.
- **NOT allowed:** partner internal *production*, running internal business operations in production, production services to customers. Internal dev/test/demo IS allowed.
- UC bought at discount is NOT eligible for incentives/rewards. Overages billed monthly at the same 55% discount.
- **Critical for existing tenancy:** UC can only be applied to environments purchased under the current or previous OPN program. UC CANNOT be applied to a regular customer/production tenancy (no mixing production and non-production). So `cps_admin@sonrisa.hu` likely needs a NEW OPN dev/test environment unless that tenancy was already secured under OPN.

**Process to order (the actual steps):**
1. Complete the **UC Request Template** (form).
2. If adding to an existing OPN environment, provide existing Subscription ID / SPM number / Contract ID.
3. Submit via the **"Submit a Ticket"** form, subject line **"Test and Dev order"**, Category: **Go-to-Market > Partner Programs**. Attach the template.
4. Partner Programs Team sends a quote → you approve → Ordering Document → order booking + environment provisioning.

## Entity Structure Decision: Principal vs. Associate

Sonrisa has two legal entities: a **Hungarian company** (HQ) and a **US company**. The Oracle opportunities are US-based, but the partnership will most likely be registered under the **Hungarian entity**.

### How OPN handles this (from onboarding guide):

- **Principal:** The main entity that signs the OPN Agreement, pays the subscription, manages benefits, Oracle Partner Credits, and Expertise designations. Typically the HQ.
- **Associate:** A wholly or majority-owned subsidiary of the Principal. Enrolls under the Principal's agreement at the same Level. The Principal must be an active member before an Associate can enroll.

### Recommended approach for Sonrisa:

1. **Register the Hungarian entity as Principal** (Level 1, USD 5,000)
2. **Register the US entity as Associate** under the Hungarian Principal (no additional subscription fee for Associates at Level 1)
3. The Principal (HU) manages credits, designations, and benefits centrally
4. The Associate (US) can operate under the same OPN agreement for US-based projects
5. CCE users can be assigned from either entity

**Important:** The Associate enrollment requires the Principal to be active first, so the Hungarian entity must complete enrollment before the US entity can join. The Associate's membership term is tied to the Principal's dates.
