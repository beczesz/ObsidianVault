## Strategic Insight

The career page scan reveals a clear pattern: **7 out of 15 pipeline companies are actively hiring DevOps/cloud/infra roles right now.** This is a much stronger signal than job board scanning alone, because:

1. **Direct career page postings** (Loxon, Chemaxon, Colossyan, CIG Pannonia, EOS Faktor) mean the company is investing in recruitment -- they have budget allocated

3. **Companies with backend-only postings** (KBOSS, Lensa) are the classic "hidden need" -- developers doing DevOps work without realizing they need a dedicated team
