## Sequence A: Cold Outreach (LinkedIn + Email)

**Target:** CTOs, VP Engineering, Heads of DevOps at companies hiring DevOps engineers or running AWS workloads.

**Trigger signals:** Job posting for DevOps/SRE role, recent funding, migration mention in blog/press, AWS-related tech stack visible.

### Day 1: LinkedIn Connection Request

> Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] is [hiring for DevOps / scaling on AWS / building cloud infrastructure]. I lead Cloud Platform Services at Sonrisa -- we help companies like yours run AWS operations without building a full internal team. Would love to connect.

### Day 3: LinkedIn Follow-up (after connection accepted)

> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I don't want to pitch you anything -- just curious: what's your biggest infrastructure challenge right now as you scale? We work with companies at a similar stage and I'm always interested in what's top of mind.

### Day 5: Email #1 -- The Value Hook

**Subject:** Quick question about [Company]'s AWS setup

> Hi [Name],
>
> I came across [Company] and noticed you're [running on AWS / hiring DevOps / scaling your platform]. Quick question: are your developers spending significant time on infrastructure work instead of product features?
>
> We help mid-sized companies run their AWS operations at a fixed monthly cost, so engineering teams can focus on building product. Our clients typically save 10-30% on AWS spend in the process.
>
> We offer a free AWS Cost Health Check -- no commitment, just a clear picture of where your cloud spend stands and what the quick wins are.
>
> Would that be useful for [Company]?
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

### Day 10: Email #2 -- Social Proof

**Subject:** How [similar company type] saved EUR X on cloud ops

> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up on my note. Wanted to share a quick example:
>
> A [similar industry/size] company was spending [X hours/month] of developer time on infrastructure. After switching to our managed service (EUR [package price]/month), they freed up their team to ship features 40% faster and reduced their AWS bill by [X]%.
>
> The math: our Growth package (EUR 4,000/month) costs less than half of hiring one senior DevOps engineer (EUR 80-120K/year), and you get a team of 2-3 engineers starting immediately.
>
> Happy to share more details. Worth a 15-minute call?
>
> [Your Name]

### Day 17: Email #3 -- The Breakup

**Subject:** Should I close the file?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I've reached out a couple of times about AWS cloud operations support for [Company]. I don't want to be a nuisance.
>
> If the timing isn't right, no worries at all. Our free AWS Cost Health Check offer stands whenever you're ready -- just reply to this email.
>
> If someone else at [Company] would be a better contact for cloud infrastructure decisions, I'd appreciate a pointer.

> [Your Name]
