### **Company Overview**

**Sonrisa Technologies** is an international software development and consultancy firm founded in **2006** by three IT professionals in Budapest, Hungary. The company specializes in building innovative, enterprise-grade software solutions with a heavy emphasis on **AI (Artificial Intelligence)** and **Cloud capabilities**.

Starting as a small boutique team, Sonrisa has grown into a robust organization with over **300 engineers** across multiple offices in Central Europe and the United States. They position themselves as a partner that combines the agility of a small team with the reliability and scale of a large enterprise, serving mid-to-large global enterprises in sectors such as healthcare, travel, finance, and e-commerce.

### **Core Philosophy: "Happy Engineering"**

At the heart of Sonrisa’s culture is the principle of **#HappyEngineering**. This philosophy is built on the belief that software engineering should be more than just a job—it should be a craft that brings satisfaction to both the creators and the users. Their approach focuses on:

- **Ingenuity & Mastery:** Delivering high-quality code that matters.
    
- **Human-Centered Collaboration:** Acting not just as an outsourcing vendor, but as an integrated part of the client's team.
    
- **Transparency & Integrity:** Maintaining open communication and clear expectations.
    

Their motto, _"Our solution today, your smile tomorrow,"_ reflects their commitment to long-term client satisfaction and practical, problem-solving technology.

### **Services & Capabilities**

Sonrisa offers services spanning the entire IT lifecycle, from initial strategy to continuous operations. Their key service areas include:

- **AI Enablement:** Beyond buzzwords, they design and deploy AI agents, knowledge platforms, and Large Language Model (LLM) solutions to automate workflows and augment decision-making.
    
- **Legacy Modernization:** They transform outdated, monolithic systems into modern, cloud-ready platforms using AI-powered discovery and standardized playbooks to ensure minimal disruption.
    
- **Cloud Platform Services:** Dedicated engineers design and operate secure infrastructures on AWS, Azure, and hybrid environments, handling everything from migration to 24/7 operations.
    
- **System Integration:** They build adaptive enterprise applications that connect disparate systems into a seamless whole, establishing a "single source of truth" for businesses.
    
- **IT Consultancy:** They provide AI-enhanced assessments and strategic guidance to help companies cut inefficiencies and make confident technology decisions.
    
- **Continuity Services:** A virtual team model that ensures business continuity through scalable support and proactive system optimization.
