Prompt: What is Vision Corner? (General Context) 

Vision Corner is Sonrisa’s internal podcast platform designed to foster open communication, transparency, and alignment across the organization. It was launched in response to a growing need for more structured, regular, and authentic dialogue between leadership and employees—especially as the company scaled rapidly and adopted more hybrid and remote ways of working. 

The core purpose of Vision Corner is to create a space where leadership can share updates on Sonrisa’s vision, mission, values, and strategic direction, while also addressing employee-submitted questions and concerns. It's not just about information—it’s about building a shared understanding and reinforcing our collective identity. 

Each episode features relaxed, in-depth conversations with company leaders, including founders and C-level executives, covering a mix of topics like recent business developments, cultural insights, operational changes, product strategy, and market shifts (including AI and innovation). The episodes often include a Q&A section where real employee questions are answered directly and transparently. 

Vision Corner is for everyone at Sonrisa—whether you're a developer, designer, project manager, or part of the support teams. It gives voice to concerns, highlights success stories, and offers a behind-the-scenes look at how decisions are made and why. 

It stands apart from traditional all-hands meetings by being recorded, accessible anytime, and delivered in a conversational format. It respects the listener's time, encourages reflection, and invites participation—making it a strategic tool for connection, clarity, and culture-building at Sonrisa.
