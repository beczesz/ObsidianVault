### 1. Introduction – Summary of the Problems

In recent times, Sonrisa has faced several challenges related to internal communication. Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, remote work has become the norm, and most employees have not returned to the office. This isolation has disrupted the flow of information, which used to spread organically by word of mouth. The company's workforce grew rapidly, which further complicated communication. Employees feel isolated and, in many cases, lack adequate information about what is happening within the company.

Furthermore, in 2023, the market appeared to stagnate, creating tension, especially due to the freeze on salary increases and the cancellation of team-building events. Rumors and myths have fostered a negative atmosphere, leading to an increasing number of resignations. Employees desire community, communication, and personal interactions, yet they still cling to the convenience of remote work.

### 2. Problem Analysis

The combination of isolation and a growing workforce has deepened the internal communication challenges. The geographical distance between teams working in different cities and countries further exacerbates the difficulties in information flow. Teams cannot easily meet in person, which hampers close collaboration. All these factors contribute to increasing distrust, frustration, and cynicism, particularly in the face of current economic and industrial challenges.

### 3. Proposed Solution: Podcast Format (Vision Corner)

A series of internal podcasts could be an effective solution to these problems. The podcast format offers flexibility, as anyone can listen to it anytime—whether during work, while exercising, or at their convenience. This makes the information easily accessible to all employees.

Through the podcast, senior leadership can share the most important company information, future plans, and respond to employee questions on a monthly basis. This interactive format allows employees to ask questions anonymously, ensuring that their concerns and fears can be directly addressed by leadership. Additionally, the podcast format facilitates smooth, live discussions, which help to build trust and foster a sense of community.

### 4. Practical Steps for Launching the Podcast

The first step is to set up a mini-studio at the Budapest site, where two or three people can sit down with professional-quality audio and video equipment. A second studio will be set up in Székelyudvarhely, and the first episodes will be created by connecting the two locations. In the future, we plan to provide similar equipment at other sites (Szeged, Subotica, Pécs) to enable broader communication.

The initial focus of the podcast will be communication with senior leadership (in an All Hands Meeting style), but over time, additional technical shows may be launched, some of which could eventually be made public, for instance, on YouTube or LinkedIn.

### 5. Timeline

The planned launch date is mid-November. The equipment is expected to arrive by early November, after which a test recording will be conducted to check the quality of the sound and video, as well as the connection between the studios. The first official recording is scheduled for mid-November, and one week prior to that, we will start collecting questions from employees.

### 6. Tasks

- Purchase and install equipment at the Budapest and Székelyudvarhely studios.
- Conduct a test recording and technical checks.
- Establish a mechanism for collecting questions, ensuring anonymity.
- Identify and engage trusted individuals who can gather questions from employees.
- Edit and prepare the podcast for monthly publication.
- Plan the installation of studio equipment at other sites.

### 7. Risks and Challenges

- Technical challenges in connecting the studios and ensuring sound and video quality.
- Gaining the trust of employees so they feel comfortable asking questions.
- Ensuring regular podcast production to maintain continuous availability.
- Possible delays in setting up the studio equipment.

### 8. Conclusion

Launching an internal podcast series is a strategic step toward improving Sonrisa's internal communication. The new format will facilitate direct communication between leadership and employees, strengthening trust and a sense of community throughout the company. The podcast offers a flexible, accessible platform that can help boost organizational morale and ensure the smooth flow of information. The first steps are planned for mid-November, so the process will soon begin.
