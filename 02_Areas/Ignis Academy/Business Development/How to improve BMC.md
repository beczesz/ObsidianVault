#### **1. Tighten Revenue Mechanics (The biggest risk)**

Your note on "Seat definition ambiguity" is the most critical `#TODO` in the document.

- **The Risk:** If you charge per "Active Learner," companies will constantly churn users out to save money. If you charge per "Seat," they may hesitate to deploy wide.
    
- **The Fix:** Define a **"Committed Band" model**.
    
    - _Example:_ Sell "Up to 500 users" for Flat Fee $X.
        
    - _Why:_ This removes the friction of the CFO approving every single new user license and stabilizes your ARR.
        
- **AI Cost Control:** You flagged "AI inference can spike". You must add a **Fair Usage Policy** to your "Revenue Streams" or "Cost Structure."
    
    - _The Fix:_ "Standard license includes X hours of AI tutoring/month. Overage billed at Y."
        

#### **2. Differentiate the "North Star"**

In v2.3, you mention the "North Star KPI is measuring the value added". This is good, but vague.

- **Refinement:** Move away from "Completion Rates" (vanity metric).
    
- **Suggestion:** Define the metric as **"Skill Application Rate"** or **"Internal Mobility Rate"** (percentage of learners who are promoted or take new internal roles). This aligns perfectly with your "Economic Buyer" motivation of "Strategic workforce development".
