# Ignis Academy

**Status:** Active, pre-seed / seed phase
**Founded:** May 2025 (idea born)
**Grant won:** November 2025 (275k EUR, 125k EUR contested)
**Location:** Odorheiu Secuiesc, Romania
**TRL:** 3-4

## What It Is
AI-driven enterprise skills development platform delivering personalized future-skills learning, transparent progress tracking, and measurable business impact. Built on 5 research pillars: Dynamic Student Profiling, Adaptive Learning, Non-Linear Learning, Human-Centered Learning, and Tutoring AI Assistant.

## Business Model
- B2B SaaS (primary) with annual subscriptions per seat
- Enterprise add-ons: AI analytics, integrations, white-labeling
- Professional services: onboarding, custom content, consulting
- Future: B2C, marketplace, certifications

## Key Metrics
- North Star: Session Quality Score (SQS) — measures actual learning value
- Supporting: MAU, SQS per user/org/course, organizational ROI
- Financial: ARR, MRR, LTV, CAC, NRR
