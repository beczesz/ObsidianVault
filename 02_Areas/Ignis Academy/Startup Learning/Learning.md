## 🧭 Founder's Startup Learning Roadmap (Tailored for You)

### **Stage 0 — Orientation (1 week)**

> Goal: Build a mental model of what a startup _really is_ — how it differs from a small business, research project, or consulting company.

**Topics**

- What defines a “startup” — scalability, product/market fit, funding path.
    
- The founder’s mindset: hypothesis-driven experimentation (Steve Blank, Eric Ries).
    
- YC Startup School overview + setup.
    

**Resources**

- 🎓 [Y Combinator Startup School — Core Lectures](https://www.startupschool.org/)
    
- 📖 _The Lean Startup_ (Eric Ries) — focus on “Build–Measure–Learn” loop.
    
- 🎧 Podcast: _How I Built This_ (Guy Raz) — listen to 3–5 founder stories and note patterns.
    

**Output**

- Create a “Startup Mental Model.md” note in Obsidian with your definitions, mindset shifts, and parallels with Ignis.
    

---

### **Stage 1 — Problem, Customer, and Validation (2–3 weeks)**

> Goal: Turn Ignis’ current vision (adaptive AI learning platform) into _validated problems and personas_.

**Topics**

- Customer discovery (Steve Blank’s Four Steps to the Epiphany).
    
- How to interview users properly (open-ended, hypothesis-driven).
    
- Pain–gain mapping, early adopter definition.
    
- Market size and segmentation (TAM–SAM–SOM framework).
    

**Resources**

- YC Startup School → “How to Find an Idea” and “How to Talk to Users” modules.
    
- Stanford eCorner → _Customer Development Talks_.
    
- Book: _The Mom Test_ (Rob Fitzpatrick).
    

**Exercises**

- Conduct 10+ semi-structured interviews with real educators, students, or EdTech buyers.
    
- Summarize key insights in a “Problem Validation Log” (Obsidian table).
    

**Output**

- Document 3 core validated user pains + hypotheses.
    

---

### **Stage 2 — Product and MVP (2–3 weeks)**

> Goal: Translate validated problems into a focused MVP narrative and metrics.

**Topics**

- MVP design: “What’s the smallest thing that delivers value?”
    
- Value proposition and user journey design.
    
- UX storytelling (wireframes, click-throughs).
    
- Early metrics (activation, retention, satisfaction).
    

**Resources**

- YC: “How to Build a Product” lecture.
    
- _Zero to One_ (Peter Thiel) — think about what Ignis does that’s _non-obvious_.
    
- _UX for Lean Startups_ (Laura Klein).
    

**Exercises**

- Define MVP scope in one page: problem → solution → user flow → success metric.
    
- Create a testable prototype (Streamlit, Frappe, or mock UI).
    
- Conduct user feedback sessions.
    

**Output**

- MVP Hypothesis Document and test results summary.
    

---

### **Stage 3 — Business Model & Traction (3 weeks)**

> Goal: Move from “we can build this” to “we can sustain and scale this.”

**Topics**

- Business model archetypes (B2C SaaS, B2B SaaS, marketplace, token-based access).
    
- Unit economics, pricing, and growth loops.
    
- Measuring traction: early metrics, retention cohorts, and value vs cost of acquisition.
    
- Distribution and marketing channels.
    

**Resources**

- YC: “How to Launch (Again and Again)” and “How to Get Users.”
    
- Book: _Lean Analytics_ (Croll & Yoskovitz) — focus on key startup metrics.
    
- Reforge essays on growth and retention.
    

**Exercises**

- Draft Ignis’ _Business Model Canvas_.
    
- Sketch three pricing hypotheses (e.g., per-token, per-course, per-institution).
    
- Simulate early user growth scenarios with real numbers.
    

**Output**

- Ignis early-stage “Traction Dashboard” — a simple Notion/Obsidian table with KPIs.
    

---

### **Stage 4 — Funding, Legal, and Operations (2–3 weeks)**

> Goal: Understand how startups raise money, organize equity, and operate leanly.

**Topics**

- Bootstrapping vs accelerators vs VC funding.
    
- How YC, Techstars, and EU innovation grants differ.
    
- Cap tables, founder equity splits, and vesting.
    
- Incorporation strategy (SRL vs C-Corp, EU funding implications).
    

**Resources**

- YC Library → “How to Raise Money” and “Equity and Legal Basics.”
    
- EU Startup Nation Standard guidelines.
    
- Book: _Venture Deals_ (Brad Feld).
    

**Exercises**

- Build Ignis’ hypothetical cap table (founders + research partners).
    
- Map the next funding path (TRL-3 → TRL-6 → seed or Horizon grant).
    

**Output**

- “Funding Strategy.md” + simplified 12-month financial runway projection.
    

---

### **Stage 5 — Leadership, Culture, and Scale (Ongoing)**

> Goal: Develop startup leadership habits that sustain growth and team coherence.

**Topics**

- Founder psychology and burnout.
    
- Building culture intentionally (values, rituals, feedback loops).
    
- Hiring for mission alignment.
    
- Founder communication and storytelling.
    

**Resources**

- YC: “How to Manage,” “How to Work Together,” “Culture and Hiring.”
    
- Book: _High Growth Handbook_ (Elad Gil).
    
- _No Rules Rules_ (Netflix culture, Reed Hastings).
    

**Exercises**

- Write Ignis’ _Culture Manifesto_ (1 page).
    
- Set up weekly founder retrospectives (like your sprint reviews).
    

**Output**

- Codified leadership and culture framework for Ignis Academy.
