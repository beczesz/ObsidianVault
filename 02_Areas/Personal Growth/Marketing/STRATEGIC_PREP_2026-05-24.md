**Three plausible audience layers, ordered by realistic reach today:**

1. **Local CEE technology operators / CTOs (warm)** — already overlaps with CPS audience. Would care about AI-native ops, agent design, vault-driven workflows.
2. **AI-builder / cognition-systems community (cold but high-fit)** — Anthropic Claude Code power-users, agent-design practitioners, knowledge-management nerds. Global, English. Substance fit is strong (BDOS), surface fit is zero today.
