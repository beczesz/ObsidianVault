# Introduction

My name is Szabolcs Becze (Szabi), I was born and live in Odorheiu Secuiesc, which is part of Transylvania, Romania. My wife Julia and I have 3 children. Julia is working as an architect and I studied Computer Science.

10 years ago, I quit my job at Garmin and decided to start my own company (ExarLabs) - not because I was unhappy with my job at Garmin, but I felt that there should be more in my professional life than just working and get paid, it should have a deeper significance and impact than just growing the profits.

Julia was already a believer, but I would describe myself as an agnostic. Nevertheless, seeing Julia's faith, I decided to start this new company together with God, testing whether He truly cares about the business world.

The first year was really tough - we used up almost all of our savings and God tested my faith (for some reason Julia was never afraid). I started to pray and ask God for guidance, and I remember the first time when I clearly heard His voice inviting me to **look for other similar companies** which are led by God. This is when I attended the GLS and saw your video. I decided to contact you, but I honestly expected no answer as you looked like unreachable movie stars from a big American production, not like a real company. Surprisingly, Casey responded and asked me if I had read the articles on the website. So I did.

Long story short, I got to know Volkert and attended the first KAW Europe in Holland. I have read the Leadership Guide multiple times and founded a Prayer group for Leaders together with Julia [photo: Leaders' Prayer]. Together with a friend (Andrew Újvári) whom I talked to you about, we translated this book and we are excited to be organizing the first KAW Event in Budapest.

My company has since grown stronger - we are now a team of 12, and we have become a Kingdom Company. Inspired by BBH, we created a board that leads together with God, an ESOP-like share structure, and many more.

The reason why we are reaching out to you is because of a calling that we received in a prophecy.

# The Prophecy

Five years ago, we received a prophecy regarding our family's calling. We were told to look for a house that we would use for God's service.

We received fairly specific instructions about the house:
- The house must be able to host guests.
- It should be in the center, yet hidden.
- It must have a well (we still do not know why).
- We should begin searching, and when we see it, we will recognize it.

This house will become a command center for our city and a place where we will host people sent by God - leaders who are seeking God and who want to learn.

# How We Began Searching

Juli and I started walking through the city and looked at several properties. Around the fifth house, we experienced a striking confirmation - it was a perfect match.

On a small plot there were two separate houses. We were told that the second house had been built for the parents of the owners, but because it is located on the side of a hill, they later realized the parents would be too old to walk up the steep slope.

It has a well, and it is truly not visible from the street, yet it is only about a five-minute walk from the city center.

When we discovered this, we contacted the owner. However, it was a very unusual situation because we did not have the money to buy the house. It became clear rather quickly that we could not afford it.

Unfortunately, we had to let it go. Instead, we purchased a smaller apartment in the city center and temporarily released the house. However, since the prophecy was still valid, we regularly went there to pray and dedicate the house to God's project.

To our surprise, the years passed and the house could not be sold at all. We spoke several times with the owner, until eventually we decided to buy it in three installments over three years.

Although humanly speaking we could not generate the money within three years, God told us that we no longer had time - we should move forward with the deal, and He would provide the money.

# The Secondary Calling

Alongside this, we also have another calling: we would like to adopt a child. (We currently have three children of our own.) We would like to start the process already this year, but our current apartment is 60 square meters and fully occupied. Unfortunately, we cannot accommodate one more person.

Our desire is therefore to pay in advance and move into our bigger house by 2027. With this in mind, we have prepared two plans.
