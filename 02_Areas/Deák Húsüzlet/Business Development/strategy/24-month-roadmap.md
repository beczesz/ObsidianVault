# Exar Labs – 24-Month Strategic Roadmap (2026–2028)

_Version: 1.1 | Last updated: 2026-03-05_


## 1. Core Strategic Principle

The central goal for Exar Labs over the next two years:

**Exit the indirect outsourcing model and deliver high-value digital systems directly to clients.**

The long-term direction is for the company to:
- use **AI-accelerated development methods**
- operate with a **small, efficient team**
- build **complex digital systems for SMBs** at competitive prices

> A small, efficient team should be capable of delivering projects that previously required large development organizations.

---

## 2. The Operating Model Shift

Exar Labs has primarily operated as an **outsourcing provider**.

Over the next two years, the model gradually transforms:

**outsourcing partner → digital systems builder → direct client relationships**

This transformation happens through **pilot projects and learning cycles**, not overnight.

---

## 3. The Three Pillars

### Pillar 1 – AI-Powered Development Efficiency

Exar Labs' most important competitive advantage will be **extreme development speed**.

Three components:
- AI development tools
- Frappe-based rapid system development
- Reusable components and templates

Goal: a small team that can build business systems extremely fast.

This efficiency makes it possible to **take on complex projects at competitive prices** — and to offer revenue share models that would be unsustainable for traditional agencies.

---

### Pillar 2 – Direct Client Projects

One of the most important tasks of the next two years: **building direct client relationships**.

Exar Labs:
- does not work as a subcontractor to other companies
- solves the problems of businesses directly

Typical projects:
- ordering systems
- ERP systems
- educational platforms
- CRM systems
- production management systems

The butcher shop pilot is the first experiment in this direction.

---

### Pillar 3 – Pilot Projects and Learning Cycles

A key element of strategic operations: **rapid experimentation**.

The company learns through low-risk pilot projects:
- about new industries
- about customer behavior
- about digital business models

Each project is a **learning cycle**. The goal isn't necessarily for every project to scale — but for the team to:
- learn fast
- develop methods
- build reusable templates

---

## 4. Education and Knowledge Sharing

Exar Labs already has infrastructure that supports knowledge sharing:
- accredited training center
- Media Műhely studio
- educational events

This area serves multiple purposes:
- deepening team knowledge
- community building
- increasing professional visibility

Long-term, this can help the company **become a professional reference point in the region**.

---

## 5. The Role of the Grant Project

The educational platform grant project plays a special role:
- provides **financial stability** during the transition
- opportunity to develop a **proprietary product**
- gives the team **startup experience**

It serves as a **learning laboratory** for the full product development process.

---

## 6. The Target State (24 Months from Now)

In two years, Exar Labs ideally will be a company that:
- works with direct clients
- uses AI-based development methods
- can rapidly build business systems
- has gained experience across multiple industries

The company may by then have:
- several successful pilot projects
- stable client relationships
- proprietary products or platforms
- strong professional reputation in the region

---

## 8. The Nature of This Strategy

This strategy is **not a rigid plan**.

The next two years are about:
- learning
- experimentation
- iteration

Decisions continuously evolve based on experience and market feedback.

The goal is not to follow a predetermined path, but to build **organizational capability that adapts quickly and recognizes new opportunities**.
