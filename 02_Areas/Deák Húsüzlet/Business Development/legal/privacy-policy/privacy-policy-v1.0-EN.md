## 2. What Data We Collect

- **Full name** — account identification and delivery
- **Email address** — Google OAuth or email/password sign-in

- **Delivery address** — order fulfillment

We do **not collect** special categories of data (health, biometric, religious, etc.). We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18.

## 3. Purpose and Legal Basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Account management | Contract performance — Art. 6(1)(b) | Name, email |
| Order processing | Contract performance — Art. 6(1)(b) | Name, address, phone |
| Home delivery | Contract performance — Art. 6(1)(b) | Address, phone |

| Firebase Analytics | Consent — Art. 6(1)(a) | Anonymous events |

We do **not use your data for marketing**. We do not perform automated decision-making or profiling.

## 4. Sharing Data with Third Parties

- **DEAK PROD SRL** — receives delivery data (name, address, phone) under a data processing agreement.
- **Google Firebase / Google LLC** — provides app infrastructure (authentication, analytics). Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **EXARGROUPS S.R.L. infrastructure** — backend runs on our own servers; data is not transferred to third parties.

We do **not sell or transfer** your data to any other third party, except where required by law.

## 5. Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to improve the user experience. This system:

- Collects anonymous, aggregated event data (e.g. which pages are visited)
- Does not collect personal identifiers
- Tracks QR-code-based traffic sources (which in-store QR code was scanned)

Analytics is activated only with your consent (cookie banner). You may withdraw consent at any time in your account settings.

## 6. Cookies

We do not use third-party marketing cookies.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Account data** — active period + 2 years after deletion
- **Order data** — 5 years (Romanian Accounting Law 82/1991)
- **Analytics data** — 14 months (Firebase default)

You may request account deletion at any time at contact@exar.ro. Data required for legal obligations will be retained as required by law.

## 8. Data Security

- All data transfers use HTTPS encryption
- Passwords are stored encrypted (hashed) — we recommend Google OAuth sign-in
- Firebase is SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certified
- Admin access is restricted to authorized personnel only

In the event of a data breach, affected users and the ANSPDCP supervisory authority will be notified within 72 hours (GDPR Art. 33-34).

## 9. Your Rights

- **Right of access (Art. 15)** — you may request a copy of your stored data
- **Right to rectification (Art. 16)** — you may request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure (Art. 17)** — you may request deletion of your data
- **Data portability (Art. 20)** — you may receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Right to object (Art. 21)** — you may object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdrawal of consent** — Analytics consent can be withdrawn at any time

You may lodge a complaint with **ANSPDCP**: https://www.dataprotection.ro
