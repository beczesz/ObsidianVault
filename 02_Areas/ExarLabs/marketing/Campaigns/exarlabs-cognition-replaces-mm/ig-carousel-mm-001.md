## Caption

Middle managers are not managers. They are information routers — moving data between people who generate it and people who need it.

For 6 months I have been running an AI operating system where specialized agents handle knowledge management, orchestration, content distribution, and more. The result: operating at the scope of a 10-person team.

The surprising part? Removing the coordination layer created clarity, not chaos.
