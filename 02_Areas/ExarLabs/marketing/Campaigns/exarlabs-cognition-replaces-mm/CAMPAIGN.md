## Campaign thesis

An AI cognition stack (like BDOS) functionally replaces classical middle management roles. The founder stays in the meaning layer — priorities, taste, final decisions. Everything structural (gathering, organizing, connecting, analyzing, timing, measuring) moves to the AI layer. This is not a productivity tool — it is a new organizational form where one person + AI handles complexity that previously required 5-15 middle managers.
