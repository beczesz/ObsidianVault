## Content

1/ Middle managers are not managers. They are human routers — moving information between people who generate it and people who need it. AI doesn't assist this function. It replaces it.

2/ Ran an experiment: 6 AI agents handling knowledge management, orchestration, cognition curation, content distribution, sales ops, and dashboards. Result after 6 months — I operate at the scope of a 10-person team. Not faster. Wider.

3/ The surprising part: removing human coordination didn't create chaos. It created clarity. When every decision leaves a trace and every insight gets auto-cross-referenced, you stop managing and start meaning-making.

4/ The uncomfortable question for every org: how much of your headcount is solving an information routing problem that now has an infrastructure solution?

## Short preview
