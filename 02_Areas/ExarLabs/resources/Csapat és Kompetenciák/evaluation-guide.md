# Evaluation Guide — 10 Questions
## Based on "The 8th Habit" by Stephen Covey

---

## General Scoring Scale (applies to all questions)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112 emergency call. Problem should be addressed to the Board. |
| 2 | Very serious issue which should be resolved RIGHT NOW. Problem should be addressed to the Board. |
| 3 | Serious problem which is needed to be addressed asap. Problem can be remediated on the team level. |
| 4 | Something is not good, needs improvement. Problem can be remediated on the team level. |
| **5** | **Average — This is the baseline, the minimum expected outcome, behavior or performance.** |
| 6 | Good or very good |
| 7 | Exceptionally good |
| 8 | This person is a role model in the company in this question |
| 9 | This person is a pioneer and a role model. Give this very very rarely, only when this person brought herself/himself or the company to a new level. |
| 10 | We keep this to God. |

> For decimal values use the dot (.), e.g. 6.5

## Q1 — Emotional Bank Account (Trust Level)
*(From "7 Habits of Highly Effective People" — P/PC Balance)*

The Emotional Bank Account symbolizes the trust level between two persons (or with yourself). When you keep a promise, help somebody or cooperate efficiently you increase the level of this account. When you break a promise, you take out from the account.

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112 |
| 2 | Very severe problems. I need to talk with the Board about it. |
| 3 | Severe problems. He/she needs to address it right away. |
| 4 | I have difficulties trusting him/her. |
| 5 | Average. Not too high not too low. |
| 6 | He/she is trustworthy most of the times. I love to work with him/her. |
| 7 | He/she is very trustworthy, has a deep and integrated character and is also very competent. |
| 8 | He/she is a role model for me, I could learn from him/her modesty and competence. |
| 9 | He/she proved his/her integrity in a very deep crisis and proved to be trustworthy. He/she is not only a role model but a pioneer. |

### Guidelines for determining the trust level

1. **You evaluate the whole person.** Try to see whether this person is trustworthy in other areas of his/her life too, not just professional life. Consider relationships outside the company, promises, etc.
2. **Is this person taking responsibility** for things around his/her environment? Is he/she giving promises?
3. **Is he/she trustworthy from both a professional point of view (competence) AND personal point of view (character)?**
4. **If he/she made a mistake, did he/she apologize for it?**
5. **Is he/she the same person when "nobody is watching"?**
6. **Is this person loyal to others who are not present?**

### Examples
1. Very reliable teammate but cheats on partner outside company → **low or very low mark** (evaluating the whole person)
2. Does not break promises because makes NO promises → **negative impact** (avoiding responsibility)
3. Strong character but technically makes many mistakes and misses deadlines → **negative impact**
4. Made obvious mistake but apologized and genuinely improved → **positive impact**
5. Watches YouTube when nobody sees it → **negative or very negative impact**
6. Gossiping behind someone's back → **negative to very negative impact, potentially Board-level**

## Q2 — P/PC Balance (Termelési sebesség & Kapacitás)
*(From "7 Habits of Highly Effective People")*

These are the only questions related with performance. We compare performance to the person's role and responsibility level. Lower production speed is not inherently bad. Problems arise only when somebody is under or overperforming themselves.

### P — Production Speed
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112, you don't know what he/she spent the time with. |
| 2 | Very slow speed, everything is delayed. |
| 3 | Slow speed, with many delays. |
| 4 | Needs more time than average to finish tasks. |
| 5 | Average production speed. Average complexity task finished in time. |
| 6 | Good speed, tasks are finished in time. |
| 7 | Very good speed. Constantly improves workstyle and quality. |
| 8 | Role model in production speed, focus and efficiency. |
| 9 | Shown a new level of production speed. |

**P Guidelines:** Check the fruit of the work (speed must come with results). High speed comes with focus and intelligence. Efficient work — finds shortcuts, ways to improve. Always look at responsibility level (Junior ≠ Senior expectations).

**P Examples:**
1. Works hard but little results → low production speed with good marketing
2. Easily distracted (e.g. YouTube) → low production speed
3. Fast but inefficient (won't use "replace all" for 1000 lines) → low production speed
4. Junior who performs like a Medior deserves more; if promoted, the same speed may score lower

### PC — Production Capacity
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112 |
| 2 | Severe problems on every step. Talk to Board. |
| 3 | Low capacity, everything goes slowly and painfully. |
| 4 | Reduced capacity, gets tired relatively easily. |
| 5 | Average production capacity. |
| 6 | Good capacity, can handle multiple tasks without burnout. |
| 7 | Very good capacity, handles many parallel tasks well. Performs well even under stress. |
| 8 | Role model in capacity and stress tolerance. |
| 9 | Incredible production capacity. |

**PC Guidelines:** Check context — personal hardships warrant more tolerance, but maintaining capacity despite them = hero. Junior expected to have lower capacity than Senior. Doing many completely different things in parallel is harder than one thing.

**PC Examples:**
1. Good capacity (6) + personal tragedy + maintains same capacity → 6+
2. Junior promoted to Medior, same capacity shown → 7- (expectation is higher)
3. Always late, always behind schedule → good indicator of reduced capacity

## Q3 — Proactivity (Proaktivitás)
*(7 Habits — Habit 1: Be Proactive)*

As contentious beings we are free to act in this world. We are not the product of our biology nor society, although these can influence us heavily. The miracle of humans is the capacity to renew their environment despite the forces around us (genes, health, luck, comfort, economic status, family, etc.). This is the opposite of victim's mentality.

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112 |
| 2 | Takes no responsibility, avoids problems, always blames others — also in private life. Board should be notified. |
| 3 | Very negative, often blaming others for his/her own faults. |
| 4 | Often negative, pessimistic without much reason. |
| 5 | Average normal proactivity. |
| 6 | Highly proactive, often comes up with new ideas and improves his/her environment. |
| 7 | Very proactive, always looks for new ways to improve everything. No cynicism, no victim's mentality. |
| 8 | Role model for proactivity, often comes up with radically new ideas. |
| 9 | This person shown a new definition of proactivity. |

### Guidelines
1. How well does this person move out of his/her comfort zone? Attitude toward unknown situations?
2. Did he/she come up with new ideas to improve culture? Proposed new systems?
3. Did he/she warn about potential problems and propose solutions?
4. Did he/she act as a victim when a challenge showed up?
5. Did he/she help others overcome problems?
6. Is he/she naïve optimistic/realistic rather than cynical pessimistic?

### Examples
1. Leader avoiding responsibility, pointing to others → suggest 5-
2. Usually negative but comes with remediation ideas → suggest 5+
3. Often critical and negative without building suggestions, often a victim → suggest 5-
4. Renews himself, tries new ways, comes up with new ideas → suggest 5+
5. Shapes culture with modesty and new ideas → 6, 7 or even 8+ (very hard, requires vision, passion, competence)

## Q4 — Begin with the End in Mind (Nagy kép látása, tervezés)
*(7 Habits — Habit 2: Begin with the End in Mind)*

Usually a task fits into a bigger picture. Doing things is knowing HOW; seeing the big picture is knowing the WHY. If somebody knows the WHY then can tolerate any HOW. A captain must have a map to navigate the ship.

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112. Lack of planning puts the company in serious danger. |
| 2 | This person's work is harmful. Not in proper position, causing frustration and harm. |
| 3 | Sometimes causes damage due to lack of understanding. |
| 4 | Often acts without knowing the goal. |
| 5 | Understands in general why and what should be done. |
| 6 | Often plans and thinks ahead before acting. Good overview of the big picture. |
| 7 | Very good in planning. Not just sees the big picture but also predicts the future. |
| 8 | Role model in planning and seeing future trends/consequences. |
| 9 | Exceptional ability to overview problems. Brings planning to a new level. |

### Guidelines
1. Does this person question the meaning of rules inside the company?
2. Does this person think about how things SHOULD be, instead of how things ARE?
3. Does this person think first, make a plan, and only then act?
4. The more responsibility one has, the more planning is needed.

### Examples
1. Starts coding architecture before having a plan → design flaws, bugs, delays → suggest 5-
2. Envisions software improvements, sees future trends, recommends against obsolete tech → suggest 6+
3. Comes up with ways to improve culture based on wise overview of company WHY → suggest 6, 7 or 7+
4. Junior: some planning required but not expected to understand full tech/culture context. Medior/Senior: must understand much better. Leader without understanding the WHY → give 5-. Leader good at seeing the end at the beginning → give 6, 7, or 7+

## Q5 — Put First Things First (Időgazdálkodás, prioritások)
*(7 Habits — Habit 3: Put First Things First)*

Tasks can be categorised by urgency and importance. The urgent tasks draw attention but are often not important. The important tasks are often not urgent. To organise time well we must put the important things first.

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112. This person is causing harm to the company. |
| 2 | Serious problems with deadlines. Board attention needed. |
| 3 | Very often deadlines are missed. Tears down trust. Little/no progress with important tasks. |
| 4 | Some problems meeting deadlines, not progressing with important non-urgent tasks. |
| 5 | Average. |
| 6 | Manages time well. Important deadlines mostly met, progresses with long-term important tasks. |
| 7 | Exceptionally manages time. Almost never misses a deadline, or reschedules quickly. |
| 8 | Role model in managing time and tasks. Progresses with important long-term tasks exceptionally well. |
| 9 | Took time management to the next level. |

### Guidelines
1. Does this person get things done in time?
2. Is this person's life progressing? (Focus on important non-urgent tasks = long-term progress)
3. Does this person have time for people? Patience = good time management.
4. Can this person say NO to unimportant tasks? To say a burning YES you need to say a gentle but confident NO to almost everything else.
5. Does this person waste time on not urgent AND not important tasks?

### Examples
1. Meets most deadlines → 5 or 6. Misses ~50% → 5-
2. Planned to learn a new technology over 6–12 months and progresses weekly → 6+. If hard/impactful → 7, 8 or even 9.
3. Says yes to everybody → filled with others' priorities, half-hearted → 5 or 5-
4. Mostly doing urgent+important tasks → gets tired → compensates with YouTube/FB → 5 or 5-. If serious → 3 or 3-.

## Q6 — Think Win-Win (Nyertes-nyertes gondolkodás)
*(7 Habits — Habit 4: Think Win-Win)*

Win-win mentality relies on an abundance mentality — the world is a place where everybody can win. Opposite of scarcity mentality. Full name: "Win-Win or No Business" — if win-win is not achievable, walk away rather than accept a bad compromise.

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112. Immediate attention needed. |
| 2 | This person damages the company. Many others are avoiding him/her. Board should be notified. |
| 3 | I am afraid to approach this person because I know I will lose. |
| 4 | I often find myself losing when I interact with this person. |
| 5 | Average. |
| 6 | You feel better off after doing something together. Often open to new suggestions. |
| 7 | Exceptionally good in win-win thinking. Willing to fight for you to make sure everybody wins. Is a fighter. |
| 8 | Role model in win-win thinking. Inspires others to think win-win as well. |
| 9 | Took win-win mentality to a whole new level. |

### Guidelines
1. Is this person willing to fight for you? Win-win requires courage AND kindness. Curious and open.
2. Aggressive in finding solutions (always wants to win) → suggests win/lose mentality.
3. Always lets others win, doesn't fight for own rights → suggests lose/win mentality.
4. Willing to lose just to make opponent lose too → 112, lose/lose mentality.

### Examples
1. "I can't solve this alone, but let's sit down and find a solution together — if we find it, I'll accept it." → 6, 7 or 7+
2. Becomes aggressive, tries to shame or intimidate → 5, 4 or even 4-
3. Always says yes, even at own expense → 5 or 5-
4. Shows resentment/bitterness, willing to lose to make others lose → 3 or 3-. Consider bringing to Board.

## Q7 — Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood (Empatikus hallgatás)
*(7 Habits — Habit 5: Seek First to Understand)*

Listening levels: #1 Passive → #2 Responsive → #3 Selective → #4 Attentive → #5 Active → #6 Empathic (non-judgemental, puts self in the other's shoes). Level 6 is the goal.

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112. This person is harming the company with his attitude. |
| 2 | Tries to dominate, force viewpoint. I feel shamed when I speak with him/her. |
| 3 | Doesn't understand me, bends my arguments to make me look foolish. |
| 4 | I often feel this person does not understand me, just waits until I finish. |
| 5 | Average. |
| 6 | Good listener. I feel understood when I speak with this person. |
| 7 | Exceptional listener. I feel completely understood. |
| 8 | Role model in listening. Can formulate my position better than I do. |
| 9 | Took listening to a new level. |

### Guidelines
1. Is this person patient and attentive, or impatient?
2. Can this person repeat what I just said?
3. Can this person strengthen my arguments (steelmanning)? Or does it make me look foolish (strawmanning)?

### Examples
1. Helps you figure out the problem by listening carefully, waits until you finish, expresses own view carefully, if you push back listens and even strengthens your side → 6, 7 or 7+
2. Only waits for you to finish to start their own viewpoint → 4 or 4-
3. Listens but only hears parts → 5 or 6
4. Not only not listening but bends arguments, you feel ridiculed → 4, 3 or even 3-

## Q8 — Synergy (Szinergia)
*(7 Habits — Habit 6: Synergize)*

Synergy is the miracle of human cooperation. Types of collaboration by result:
- Destructive: 1 + 1 = -1 or less
- Compromise: 1 + 1 = 1.5
- Cooperative: 1 + 1 = 2
- **Synergistic: 1 + 1 = 3, 5, or 100**

True synergy requires total openness to the other person's suggestions and accepting that a 3rd alternative could exist that is better than everyone's original idea. Requires courage, discipline, and mutual respect.

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112. Immediate action, this person is dangerous to everyone. |
| 2 | Dangerously destructive. Board's attention is needed. |
| 3 | We had destructive cooperation, can't solve even the easiest tasks together. |
| 4 | I often find myself in unnecessary compromises when dealing with him/her. |
| 5 | Average. No synergy experienced, or cooperation is 1+1=2. |
| 6 | Good synergy experienced. We often start thinking about 3rd alternatives and find one. |
| 7 | Exceptionally good synergy. In important matters we found together a much better solution than either of us would have suggested. |
| 8 | Role model in synergy. When we find solutions together we almost always come up with something better. |
| 9 | Took synergy to a new level at the company. |

### Guidelines
1. Prerequisites of synergy are win-win mentality AND listening. Do you experience both? Good indication of synergy.
2. In a practical, concrete way — did you and this person actually create a better solution? Not just a dream but a real improvement?
3. Signs of synergy: nobody owns an idea, time "flies", you can say stupid ideas without shame, the 3rd alternative is actually better than anyone's initial idea.

### Examples
1. Ivo's magic theater was a perfect example of synergy.

## Q9 — Sharpen the Saw (Önmegújítás)
*(7 Habits — Habit 7: Sharpen the Saw)*

People can do unbelievable things — but most don't believe in their own greatness. Continuous renewal of just 0.01% daily creates exponential growth through compound interest. Renewal happens in 4 dimensions: **Physical, Intellectual, Emotional, Spiritual.**

### Score Descriptions
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | 112. This person is in great danger — maybe to others too. Board should be notified. |
| 2 | Seriously deteriorated since last evaluation. Board should be notified. |
| 3 | Deteriorated unnecessarily on some dimensions. |
| 4 | Stagnated since last evaluation. What a waste. |
| 5 | Acceptable. |
| 6 | Could renew him/herself in some important dimensions. |
| 7 | Renewed exceptionally well in multiple dimensions. Inspiring others. |
| 8 | Role model in self-renewal. Inspiring many. |
| 9 | Brought self-renewal to a whole new level. Incredible. |

### Guidelines
1. Is this person living a balanced life, trying new things across all 4 dimensions? → Good path for sustainable growth.
2. Focused on only one dimension while others stagnate or decline → won't work long term.
3. Makes plans but fails periodically → goal too big OR not enough continuous effort.

### Examples
1. Plans weekly for all 4 dimensions (eating, working out, learning, meaningful tasks) and carries them out → 6, 7 or 7+
2. Focused only on working out, good progress, but nothing else changing (or declining) → 5 or 6 (less if decline is big)
3. Decides to change many things, fails after 3 weeks, retries and fails again → 5 or 5-
