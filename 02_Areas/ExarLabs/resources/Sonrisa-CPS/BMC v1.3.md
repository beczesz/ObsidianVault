# 1. Customer Segments 

CPS targets a diverse range of businesses that rely on cloud infrastructure and DevOps capabilities but vary in their internal resources and operational needs. Each segment has specific pain points that CPS addresses through its scalable, flexible, and cost-effective service model. Below is a detailed breakdown: 

## Mid-Sized Enterprises (Tech, SaaS, E-Commerce, Finance, Healthcare, etc.) 

**Characteristics**:  
- Typically 50-500 employees, with growing cloud infrastructure needs.  
- Operate on platforms like AWS or Azure but lack a dedicated DevOps team.  
- Focused on operational efficiency, uptime, and scalability as they expand. 

**Pain Points:**  
- Struggle with CI/CD efficiency, infrastructure scaling, and cost management.  
- Developers often double as infrastructure managers, leading to inefficiencies and technical debt.  
- Need ongoing monitoring and maintenance to ensure reliability without hiring additional staff. 

**CPS Solution:**  
- Support Packages: Offers a managed DevOps team at a fraction of the cost of full-time hires (Growth Package at €4,000/month with 80h, Scale Package at €6,000/month with 120h).  
- Cloud Optimization: Provides scalability, cost efficiency, and performance improvements through proactive monitoring and expertise.
  
Value: Reduces operational burden and technical debt, enabling focus on core business growth. 

## Startups & Scale-Ups 

**Characteristics:**  
- Small teams (5-50 employees) with limited budgets and rapid growth trajectories.  
- Early adopters of cloud solutions but lack in-house DevOps expertise.  
- Prioritize fast product development cycles and cost control. 

**Pain Points:**  

- Cannot afford a full-time DevOps team, leading to reliance on developers for infrastructure.  
- Need scalable cloud environments to support growth but face security and cost overrun risks.  
- Require quick, expert guidance to avoid early-stage inefficiencies.
**CPS Solution:**  
- Essential Package (€2,000/month with 40h): Provides foundational DevOps support and on-demand expertise.  
- Cloud Migration & Modernization: Transitions startups to production-ready setups.  
- FinOps: Prevents runaway cloud costs with optimization strategies.  

Value: Enables startups to scale quickly and securely without large upfront investments. 

## Large Enterprises (Seeking External Cloud & DevOps Expertise) 

**Characteristics:**  
- 500+ employees, often with established in-house DevOps teams.  
- Operate complex, multi-cloud environments requiring advanced management.  
- Focus on compliance, security, and operational resilience. 

**Pain Points:**  
- Internal DevOps teams are overloaded or lack specific skills for overflow work.  
- Seek third-party validation for security audits, cost optimization, and cloud strategy.  
- Need 24/7 availability for critical infrastructure support. 

**CPS Solution:**  
- Scale & Custom Support: Offers flexible, enterprise-grade teams (Scale package at €6,000/month with 120h or custom pricing for larger enterprises) with tailored SLAs and optional 24/7 On-Call Support addon (€2,000/month).
- Consulting & Projects: Provides cloud migrations, Kubernetes optimization, and hybrid cloud setups.  
- FinOps & DevSecOps: Ensures cost efficiency and compliance with ongoing support.  
  
Value: Augments internal teams, reduces risk, and ensures compliance and uptime. 
    

## Companies with Existing DevOps Teams Needing Backup Support 

**Characteristics:**  
- Small to mid-sized businesses with 1-3 DevOps engineers already in place.  
- Concerned about redundancy and knowledge silos tied to a single person.  
- Operate stable cloud setups but need occasional support. 

 **Pain Points:**  
- Risk of operational disruption if their DevOps engineer leaves or is unavailable.  
- Overloaded internal teams need help with maintenance, security reviews, or cost analysis.  
- Want to avoid downtime or delays due to lack of backup. 

**CPS Solution:**  
- Safety Net (€990/month): Acts as a safety net with standby expertise and 6 hours/month of consultation.  
- Essential (€2,000/month with 40h): Offers a hybrid model collaborating with in-house teams for ongoing management.  

Value: Provides redundancy, reduces single-person dependency, and enhances operational stability.
    

## Future Expansion Segments 

### Regulated Industries (Finance, Healthcare, Government):  
- Characteristics: Require strict compliance (e.g., SOC2, PCI DSS, ISO 27001) and secure cloud operations.  
- Needs: Robust DevSecOps and compliance automation.  
- CPS Potential: Tailored Custom Support with security add-ons to meet regulatory demands. 
    
### AI/ML & Data-Intensive Companies:  
- Characteristics: Use high-compute cloud resources for AI, machine learning, or big data.  
- Needs: Inference Farm on their own cloud.
- CPS Potential: Setup and manage LLM inference on their custom infrastructure
    

### Companies Transitioning from On-Premise to Cloud:  
- Characteristics: Traditional businesses moving to AWS/Azure.  
- Needs: Migration expertise and full cloud adoption support.  
- CPS Potential: One-time project services with a path to ongoing Support Packages. 
    
Value for Future Segments: Positions CPS as a scalable partner for emerging markets with high growth potential. 
    

Summary of Key Pain Points & CPS Solutions 

| Customer Segment            | Key Pain Point                     | CPS Solution                          |

| Mid-Sized Enterprises       | No dedicated DevOps team           | Managed DevOps at lower cost          |

CPS caters to businesses at various stages of cloud adoption, from resource-constrained startups to sophisticated enterprises, with a clear path for future growth into regulated and high-compute sectors. This segmentation ensures broad market coverage while addressing specific, actionable needs with tailored support packages.
