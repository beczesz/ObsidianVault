## Strategic Context: Your Unfair Advantages

Before executing, understand why CPS can win:

### Strengths to Leverage

- **300-Engineer Bench:** Pull specialized expertise (AI/ML, legacy modernization) that 10-person MSPs cannot match
- **No Single Points of Failure:** Talent redundancy eliminates the risk that kills small MSPs
- **Enterprise DNA:** SOC2/ISO27001 posture + Lufthansa/Oracle audit history = 6-12 month shortcut vs. competitors
- **Service Bundling:** "Migrate + Manage" at blended rates 30-40% cheaper than hiring separate vendors
- **Financial Resilience:** 19 years in business, can offer Net-30/60 terms

### Weaknesses to Address

- **AWS Partnership Lag:** Select Tier = invisible to AWS Enterprise Sales steering $50K+ deals
- **Positioning Ambiguity:** Market may perceive CPS as "software company doing cloud" side hustle
- **Price Compression Risk:** €50/hour blended vs. Indian MSPs at €25-35/hour — must defend on quality, not price

### Strategic Implication

Your differentiation is **integrated delivery**: "We'll modernize your legacy app, migrate to AWS, manage it 24/7, AND build AI-powered cost optimization." No small MSP can make this promise. Lead with this.

## Sales Motion: The Wedge-to-Moat Model

### The Economics

|Path|Revenue|Margin|Annual Profit|
|---|---|---|---|
|Migration Project Only|€40,000|15%|€6,000|
|Migration → CPS Conversion|€40,000 + €48,000/year|15% + 40%|€6,000 + €19,200|

**Insight:** The CPS conversion yields **3.2x the project profit annually**. Projects are wedges; CPS is the moat.

### Qualification Criteria

Before investing sales effort, confirm:

- AWS spend ≥$5,000/month (or production workload)
- No dedicated DevOps team (or team is overloaded/single-person risk)
- Near-term trigger (release, migration, compliance deadline)

### Pricing Strategy

|Scenario|Price|Margin|Rationale|
|---|---|---|---|
|Migration (New Client)|€35-45K|15-20%|Win deal; CPS upsell is profit center|
|Migration (Existing Client)|€25-35K|25%|Lower acquisition cost|
|Standalone CPS|€2K-6K/month|40%+|Full rate — no project wedge|
|Project + CPS Bundle|10% discount on CPS Year 1|35%|Incentivize 12-month commitment|

### Conversion Tactics

**During Project (Months 1-3):**

- Include "3 months post-migration CPS Essential support" in SOW
- Track actual hours used vs. allocated

**Conversion Meeting (Month 4):**

- "You've used 35 of 40 hours/month on average. Here's what breaks if we stop..."
- Show specific value delivered (incidents prevented, cost saved)
- Offer 10% annual prepay discount
