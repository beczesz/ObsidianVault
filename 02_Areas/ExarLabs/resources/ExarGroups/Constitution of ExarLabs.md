# Constitution of ExarLabs

1. ## Our Mission

**Build, Grow, and Impact.**

**Build:** We build software solutions for companies with the talents provided us by God. We work with discipline, passion, and excellence.
**Grow:** In the ever-changing world we constantly renew and improve ourselves and our company culture.
**Impact:** As an organization, we show an example, opened to share what we already live. We are also serving others outside of the company with our available resources.

2. ## Our Vision

***Our vision is to become a fortress built on a rock which cannot be hidden:***

#### Built on rock

Our strength comes from God and His righteousness. We maintain a culture that is based on trust and abundance mentality.

#### Fortress

We maintain a culture where everybody understands and follows the same vision. We are organized having transparent systems and we empower our employees to do what they are the best in. We are the army of the fortress.

#### Cannot be hidden

As we are visible to everybody we inspire with our example impacting other companies and people. We are blessed to be a blessing. We will intentionally look for ways to share our time, talents, and treasures with others.

3. ## Company Culture

### Relational culture

We support the mental and physical health of every employee, we maintain a culture of sharing and respect with honest relationships. We believe that everyone has something valuable which can be learned. Not just our professional life, but our personal life too must be in accordance with this constitution.

The worker deserves his wages, and our goal is to pay our members well. Only a well-paid employee can be motivated, can feel important, and can provide safety to his/her family. Similarly, we believe that family is a precious value and it is superior to wealth and money. We are supporting and encouraging employees with children, helping mothers to integrate easier back to work after maternity leave.

### Purpose oriented culture

We are **purpose-oriented** and **not profit-oriented**. Everything we do should have a crystal clear purpose. This purpose must be constitutional and agreed upon by everybody, and it is **not** to maximize the profit. Each employee should spend their time working on meaningful projects which make sense both for the company and for the employee. The company should use its profit to make a meaningful contribution to society which motivates every member as well.

As an IT company, we have more influence and power on the economy than other companies from other fields. As long as this is true, we are more responsible for others and for everybody and we are doubly accountable for how we use our profit and potentials.

### Aligned culture

In order to do excellent work, we maintain an interior discipline and clarity in how we work. Our rules and systems are transparent so everybody every time knows how he/she can contribute to the common goal.

To be more competent as an IT company in the Information Age we should be constantly learning, getting aware of new technologies, and be opened to use them.

We should also help other non-technical people/companies to easily integrate, learn, and use new technologies.

### Empowered culture

We believe in guided autonomy, where the teams get increased freedom and responsibility to decide how to resolve the given problem.

4. ## Our Values

### Proactivity and Responsibility

Instead of reacting to, or worrying about conditions over which we have little or no control, we focus our time and energy on things we can control. We are taking responsibility for our given word, work, and promises and we are accountable for them.

ExarLabs is organized hierarchically, where everybody should be proactive and take as much responsibility as possible, on their level. Based on the hierarchical level the responsibility from bottom to top is increasing, but in the end, the leaders are the ones who take full responsibility.

### Self-discipline and self-guidance

A soul cannot bear freedom without self-discipline. We believe that in order to have true freedom and autonomy we must train ourselves with self-discipline not to deviate from our tasks.

### Win/Win Mindset

We believe in a win-win mindset, where both parties can be winners at the same time. We will always try to avoid cases where we make others lose by making us win. We maintain an abundance mentality, where everybody has enough to feel a winner. If no such win/win situation can be achieved then we would go for no business. To achieve this it is enough if only one party thinks win/win.

### Understanding the other first

We see things from another's point of view before sharing our own. Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to become angry.

### Synergy

Synergizing is the habit of creative cooperation. It's a process, and through that process, people bring all their personal experience and expertise to the table. Together, they can produce far better results than they could individually. It is the idea that the whole is greater than the sum of the parts. One plus one equals three, or six, or sixty.

### Self-renewal

We constantly try to renew ourselves in the four key areas of our life: physical, intellectual, emotional, and spiritual.

### Integrity and honesty

Honesty is telling the truth — in other words, conforming our words to reality. Integrity is conforming reality to our words — in other words, keeping promises and fulfilling expectations, according to our roles. This requires an integrated character, an oneness, primarily with self but also with life.
Integrity also means that you are loyal to those who are not present.

### Servant leadership

We believe that to be a leader is a privilege, which should be taken with modesty. The highest priority of a servant leader is to encourage, support, and enable subordinates to unfold their full potential, abilities, and character.
Every member must agree and accept this constitution and **should try** to respect it.

5. ## Services and relationship with clients

A client is a person or organization which requests our services. Somebody can only become our client if their needs are ethical and respect this constitution.
Our clients are not our masters. We should serve them, we should give them our time, energy, and creativity, we should solve their problems but only if we consider that it is ethical and respects this constitution.

We believe that the given word must be kept and if we promise something we should stick to our promise. We have to earn our client's trust and we want to be acknowledged as a fully professional company.

# Appendix

1. ## ExarLabs meaning

Exar comes from two Latin words: **Ex**ercitus (Army) **Ar**ce (Citadel).
Citadel symbolizes our company which is strong and disciplined enough so it can go against the current and invests its profit into its environment, supporting life, giving financial stability to as many people as possible.
The citadel needs an army, which must be formed out of trained and powerful soldiers.
This name symbolizes that we are the army of the citadel.
