## Page Layout

- **Page size:** A5 (417.6 x 594 pt / ~148 x 210 mm)
- **Full-bleed dark backgrounds** on title/cover pages (#2B525E)
- **Background images** used on cover and section openers (full page, under text)
- **Callout bars** in dark teal across the page for sections like "READING", "WATCH THIS VIDEO"

## Design Patterns

- **Cover pages:** Full dark bg + large Oswald title (white) + gold subtitle
- **Section openers:** Full dark bg image + large Oswald title + gold module label + page number
- **Content pages:** Light/white bg + dark teal body text + gold accents for module refs
- **Callout sections:** Dark teal horizontal bar spanning page width, white Oswald text inside
- **Text hierarchy:** ALL CAPS for titles, labels, and section names
- **Quotes:** Proxima Nova Regular Italic, dark teal

## Spacing & Structure

- Generous margins on content pages
- Clear visual separation between sections using full-page dark backgrounds
- Consistent header/footer on content pages with breadcrumb style: "ACTION GROUP WORKBOOK | MODULE ONE | SPIRITUAL IDENTITY"
