## EP17 Summary (previous episode)
Solo episode (~60 min), 30 practical AI tips organized around 6 human thinking frameworks:
1. Humans think by talking (outsourced thinking via conversation)
2. Creative mode vs. editor mode (flow state vs. structuring)
3. Breadth-first vs. depth-first learning
4. Mind map method for visual understanding
5. Multiple perspectives and argumentation
6. Analogies and metaphors for comprehension

Selected tips covered: separate chats by topic, name chats well, learn the platform's features (voice, image, PDF), build context first, content before form, create surveys/questionnaires, use email source code, mind maps, multiple AI models in dialogue (ChatGPT + Grok), metaphors/analogies, learning with summaries, personalized study plans, language learning, interview prep, generate tips.

Core message of EP17: "Everything mechanical in us, AI will simulate. Everything human in us, it won't replace."

## EP42 Angle (this episode)
Deeper/philosophical angle — focus on what is uniquely human, organized around **4 human dimensions**:
- **Fizikai / Felelősség vállalás** (Physical / Responsibility) — Ethos
- **Intellektuális / Értelem, összefüggések** (Intellectual / Meaning) — Logos
- **Érzelmi / Empátia** (Emotional / Empathy) — Pathos
- **Spirituális / Vágyak, küldetés tudat** (Spiritual / Purpose) — Thelos

Principles guiding the episode:
1. Practical tips — concrete examples that spark thinking
2. Human at the center, not AI
3. Timeless principles (not trend-chasing)
4. Logical structure following the 4 human dimensions
