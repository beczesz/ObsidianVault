# End of the Exponential

## Definition

Dario Amodei's phrase for the approaching inflection point where AI scaling (the exponential growth in capability driven by compute, data, and improved training) either saturates or transitions into a qualitatively different paradigm. As of Feb 2026, Amodei argues we are near this point — not meaning progress stops, but that the current exponential *phase* (from high-school-level to PhD-level model capability) is nearly complete.

The phrase is carefully chosen: it describes the *end of this particular exponential curve*, not the end of AI progress. The next phase (autonomous professional-level agents, then potential recursive self-improvement) may follow different dynamics.

**Empirical grounding:** Model capabilities have advanced on schedule — from high-school-level reasoning (2020) → college-level (2022) → PhD/professional-level in mathematics and code (2024–2025). The remaining gap is end-to-end task integration (project management, ambiguous requirements), which Amodei frames as 1–3 years away.

**Sociological observation:** Amodei regards the public's failure to register this trajectory as the most surprising development of the past three years — the communication gap between AI researchers and broader society is historically unusual given the stakes.
