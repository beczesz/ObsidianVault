# Amodei vs LeCun on Scaling

## Dario Amodei (Anthropic)

Scaling compute + data + improved objective functions is sufficient to reach AGI. The "Big Blob of Compute" hypothesis holds: algorithmic cleverness matters far less than the seven core factors. RL now follows the same log-linear scaling laws as pre-training, confirming the approach is generalisable. Human-level general intelligence is 1–3 years away via this path. Evidence: consistent benchmark progression from high-school → college → PhD-level reasoning over 2020–2025.

## Yann LeCun (Meta AI)

Current architectures (large language models, transformers) cannot produce AGI because they lack the ability to build *world models* — internal causal representations of how the physical and social world works. Sample efficiency is not merely a scaling issue; it reflects a fundamental architectural gap. A truly general learner would not require billions of dollars of data and bespoke RL environments to learn how to use Excel. Scaling the wrong architecture just produces very capable pattern-matchers, not general intelligence.

## Overlaps

Both agree compute matters. Both agree current models are impressive and practically useful. Both believe AGI is an important goal. Both agree safety matters.

## Differences

| Axis | Amodei | LeCun |
