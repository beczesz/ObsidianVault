Comparing Hemingway’s concise tale of Santiago’s struggle with Melville’s sprawling epic *Moby‑Dick* highlights different approaches to man‑versus‑nature narratives: Hemingway distills existential themes into a short, symbolic battle with a marlin, while Melville explores obsession and defiance through Captain Ahab’s relentless pursuit of the white whale.
