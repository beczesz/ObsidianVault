Contrasting Hemingway’s novella with his earlier novel *A Farewell to Arms* reveals how different contexts shape the portrayal of courage and loss: *The Old Man and the Sea* focuses on an individual’s solitary battle with nature and the nobility of endurance, whereas *A Farewell to Arms* explores love and disillusionment amid the collective trauma of war.
