## Folder Purpose
`03_Resources/02_Books` — the book library of the Ideas Vault. Each book gets its own subfolder with:
- A main summary `.md` (speed-reader output)
- `Atomic_Ideas/` — standalone concept notes
- `Contrasts/` — comparison notes between books
- Sometimes a source PDF or ePub
