02 — Approach

Mass & Void

Every project begins the same way: we decide what is solid and what is left open. The rest, material and detail, follows that first decision.

  1. 01

    Read the site before the brief

    Orientation, light, neighbours, and history set more constraints than any client wish list. We measure those first.

  2. 02

    Subtract until it argues

    A building should make a single clear claim. We remove everything that blurs it, then defend what remains.

  3. 03

    Detail is not decoration

    A junction, a reveal, a shadow gap. The smallest moves carry the idea down to the scale of a hand on a rail.

  4. 04

    Build to outlast the trend

    We choose materials that age into something better, so the building is more convincing in thirty years than on opening day.

03 — Studio

A small studio, on purpose.

OBLIQUE was founded in Lisbon in 2009 by Mara Oliveira and Tomás Réti. We have stayed deliberately small, fourteen people, so that a partner sits at every table from the first sketch to the last site visit.

We work slowly, take few projects at a time, and would rather decline a commission than dilute it. The buildings on this page are the ones we chose to keep.
