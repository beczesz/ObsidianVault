## 1. THE NEWSLETTER — UNPACKED

### Core Argument Chain
1. Pageau and Peterson co-authored a 10,000-word paper for ARC
2. Central thesis: **subsidiarity** is the answer to the meaning crisis at the societal level

- **80 years of blinders** since WWII: Western societies suppressed strong identity
- We preferred "the side-kicks, the betas, the rag-tag team of misfits" — the marginal over the central

## 2. THE ARC PAPER — FULL DEEP EXPLORATION

**Title:** "Identity: Individual and the State versus the Subsidiary Hierarchy of Heaven"
**Authors:** Jonathan Pageau and Jordan Peterson
**Published:** ARC Research Paper + First Things (Oct 31, 2023)
**Context:** Presented at ARC conference, London, Oct 30 – Nov 1, 2023
**Length:** ~10,000 words

### What is ARC?
The Alliance for Responsible Citizenship, co-founded by Jordan Peterson and Baroness Philippa Stroud, backed by the Legatum Institute. A global movement offering a positive alternative vision for society. First conference: London, October-November 2023. Next: London, June 2026.

### Master Thesis
> "Our identity is fractal, hierarchical, subsidiary. Proper responsible participation in all its levels provides life with purpose, meaning, security, hope, and adventure."

---

### SECTION A: THE PROBLEM — The Identity Crisis

The paper opens with a diagnostic of modern identity collapse:

> "The question of identity haunts us, underlying our political excesses and extremes. As individuals, in the absence of a firm identity, we become adrift."

The modern false binary: identity has been collapsed into a duality between the idiosyncratic individual and the ever-totalising collective. These two tendencies grow SIMULTANEOUSLY — worship of particularity/exception on one hand, growing bureaucratic state on the other.

> "Whether individualists or collectivists, we often act as if these two vectors of identity are in competition with each other."

**The key move:** This is NOT a competition. They are co-dependent. More atomized individuals REQUIRE more encompassing state structures. More fragmentation generates demand for totalizing unity.

**The erosion of intermediary structures:**
> "The result has been the slow but persistent erosion of intermediary identities: the family, communities, religious affiliations, clubs, and the nation — as the individual sees these intermediary identities as constraining his or her freedom. The growing collective sees these intermediary participations as impure visions of itself, competing with its own totalising identity. We are left with hopeless and lonely individuals facing an increasingly controlling and invasive state."

---

### SECTION B: BIBLICAL IMAGERY — The Whore and the Scarlet Beast

The paper maps Revelation 17 onto modern political/cultural pathologies:

**The Beast** (the totalitarian state, the Leviathan):
> "civilisation and its leaders, the colour of earth and blood... a living Tower of Babel, the arrogant state/king and its vassals, hellbent on total subordination."

**The Whore** (atomized individualism, licentiousness):
> "freedom of anonymity," "the dissociation of sex from the constraints and guides of tradition," and "the dissolution of constraint that accompanies atomized individuality."

**The Dynamic:** The Whore RIDES the Beast (Rev 17) — the state uses licentiousness to lure people into its absolute control.

**The Beast Always Kills the Whore:**
The trajectory is always the same — the totalizing state promises freedom but destroys even desire itself. Historical examples:
- The anarchy of the French Revolution → Napoleonic empire
- The hedonism of Weimar Germany → the totalitarian Third Reich
- The free West → technocratic authoritarianism during COVID

**Hobbesian vs. Lockean/Rousseauian mapping:**
Modern society is split between Hobbesian ideology (the Beast, state supremacy) and Lockean/Rousseauian ideology (the Whore, radical individual freedom) — or a perverse mixture of both.

---

### SECTION C: THE THIRD VISION — The New Jerusalem

Revelation offers a third picture — neither Beast nor Whore:
> "a city of peace, descending from Heaven, aligned toward the greatest good, God Himself."

The New Jerusalem, embodied by Christ, is the positive alternative to both tyrannical unity AND dissolute fragmentation. This is the model for subsidiarity.

---

### SECTION D: THE SUBSIDIARITY PRINCIPLE

Rooted in Pope Leo XIII's Rerum Novarum (1891), further defined in Pius XI's Quadragesimo Anno (1931).

**The formal definition (direct quote from the paper):**
> "In a properly structured subsidiary system, the individual voluntarily adopts responsibility for his or her conduct and caretaking, but the couple has its domain and duty, as does the family, the neighbourhood, the corporate enterprise, the city, and the state. The more encompassing levels are restricted in their domain of purview to those actions and directions of attention that cannot be taken up by lower and more proximal levels of the hierarchical structure."

**The levels of the hierarchy (ascending):**
1. The individual
2. The couple
3. The family
4. The neighbourhood
5. The corporate enterprise / community
6. The city
7. The nation / state
8. Religious communion
9. The transcendent Good itself (God)

---

### SECTION E: FRACTAL IDENTITY — The Inward-Outward Mirror

This is the paper's most original philosophical contribution:

> "Subsidiary identity is understanding that as individuals, we are already a bringing into one of all the different thoughts, feelings, and psychological micro-personalities within us. It is our very capacity to join the multiple into one which becomes a mirror of how we are parts of higher, broader identities."

**The fractal logic:** The same pattern of "many uniting into one" REPEATS at every level. The individual's internal psychological integration mirrors the family's integration into community, which mirrors the city's integration into nation, which mirrors the nation's orientation toward the transcendent.

> "Rather than opposing that unity to an abstract collective, it is our very capacity to join the multiple into one that becomes a mirror of how we are parts of higher identities, not an abstract collective, but rather dancing within a cascade of unities."

**Each level has genuine ontological status:**
> "So too, our families are themselves unified agents in the building of cities, and our unified cities are real identities forming nations, with each level existing as its own level of reality, autonomy, but ultimately always giving itself up into higher participations."

**Government is necessary but NOT the highest level:**
> "In this vision, we soon realise that the highest vision of identity and participation is not government, though governments are necessary, but in the very virtues which make it possible to exist together in harmony in the first place, and ultimately the transcendent Good itself."

**The upward directionality:**
> "The subsidiary model always leads us higher. By always aligning our vision beyond particularities, the very ones we unapologetically celebrate, by aiming toward virtue, but also in full knowledge of difference, of strangers, and of exceptions — that is, in compassion to those who do not fit with our ideals — that is how we maintain our subsidiary identities in service of the highest Good."

---

### SECTION F: CITIZENSHIP REDEFINED

> "To be a citizen is not to be a citizen of an abstract collective; it is to be a parent, a friend, a neighbour."

> "It is not in the constant suspicion of any common identity, but it is in celebrating and remembering our immediate bonds, our stories and rituals, our holidays, our monuments, that we can be anchored properly in the world."

---

### SECTION G: THE EXISTENTIAL PAYOFF — Meaning Restored

The paper's climax:
> "Such an approach is the medication for existential angst, the source of the hope that abides. It is the shining star, beckoning in the distance. It is the only true alternative to lonely wandering in the desert and slave-like subjection to tyranny. It is the ultimate sacrificial gesture, the offering of our little stories to the harmony of the whole."

**This is the answer to meaning:** participation in the subsidiary hierarchy — from the couple to the transcendent — is what rescues individuals from both nihilistic atomization AND totalitarian subjection. The offering of the personal to the larger whole (at each level) is the sacred gesture.

### SECTION H: CRITIQUES OF THE PAPER

**Source:** Ron Rivers, "Critiques of 'State Versus the Subsidiary Hierarchy of Heaven'" (Medium / Age of Awareness, Jan 31, 2024)
**Source:** JSB Morse, The Libertarian Catholic (Nov 1, 2023) — sympathetic but critical

**Critique 1 — The "Hierarchy of Heaven" is not neutral:**
Grounding a political framework in explicitly Christian cosmology excludes non-Christians and non-theists. The paper presents a sectarian vision as a universal solution.

**Critique 2 — Subsidiarity historically enabled oppression:**
Letting local units handle their own affairs has historically protected local discrimination (racial segregation, domestic abuse, local religious persecution). The paper treats subsidiarity as inherently benevolent without engaging this dark history.

**Critique 3 — Intermediate structures can themselves oppress:**
The paper focuses on the state as the threat, but the "subsidiary" units (family, community, church) can themselves be sites of profound oppression — especially for women, minorities, and LGBTQ+ individuals. The hierarchy provides no remedy for this.

**Critique 4 — Conflating descriptive and normative claims:**
The fractal structure is presented as both a natural/cosmological FACT and a political PRESCRIPTION. That reality has hierarchical structure does not mean that any particular hierarchy is good or just.

**Critique 5 — The WWII critique as historical revisionism:**
Pageau's framing treats anti-fascism as a pathology rather than a rational response.

**Critique 6 — ARC's institutional context:**
The conservative political funding (Legatum Institute) and Peterson's co-founding role suggest the paper functions as ideological scaffolding dressed in philosophical language.

**Critique 7 (Morse, sympathetic) — Hubris without God:**
Even accepting the Pageau/Peterson framework, ARC's civic framing is insufficient. Without deliberate attention to the transcendent, we can at best turn the clock back a few decades. The solution requires "conversion of our collective soul," not just structural reorganization.

## 3. DEEP PHILOSOPHICAL ANALYSIS

### 3.1 WWII as Founding Myth
Pageau's most provocative thesis: every civilization has a "sacred origin." For the post-war West, that myth is WWII itself. The Nazis became the absolute evil, the ultimate "axis" (axis = center, identity). Therefore ANY strong identity, ANY axis, ANY claim to a center became suspect. We built an entire civilization on AVOIDING being "the bad guys." This produced the permanent preference for the margin over the center. But myths decay — and as WWII generation dies, the myth loses binding power. Raw identity hunger emerges.

### 3.2 The Meaning Crisis Connection
John Vervaeke's "Awakening from the Meaning Crisis" traces the same problem from cognitive science. Pageau approaches through SYMBOLISM. They converge: when you lose symbolic literacy, you lose the capacity to experience meaning. Modern secularism didn't just remove God — it removed the entire symbolic grammar that made coherence possible. The "flattening" in the newsletter IS the loss of symbolic hierarchy. Subsidiarity is not just political — it's COSMOLOGICAL.

### 3.3 The Pendulum Metaphor
- **Arm 1 (where we've been):** Mercy, excess, comfort, looseness, the feminine. The "whore" archetype.
- **Arm 2 (where we're heading):** War, rigour, asceticism, conflict, the masculine. The "beast" archetype.
- **The Center (subsidiarity):** Not atomized individual (too loose), not totalitarian state (too rigid), but nested hierarchy of care.

Maps onto: Mercy vs. Justice | Chaos vs. Order | Feminine vs. Masculine | Many vs. One

### 3.4 "Know Your Role" — echoes vocation, medieval estates, St. Paul's Body of Christ (1 Cor 12), Hindu dharma/svadharma. Identity without PLACE is chaos.

### 3.5 Summit Context
Summit frames Christianity as "an epic, not just lyrics." Mary Harrington's presence significant — her "reactionary feminism" IS subsidiarity applied to gender. When you remove the local (family, community), the global (market, state) takes over.

## 4. KEY INSIGHTS

1. **Individualism and totalitarianism are co-dependent.** More atomized individuals REQUIRE bigger states. Subsidiarity breaks this cycle.
2. **WWII as founding myth explains 80 years of Western identity-phobia.** The allergic reaction to any "axis" was mythological response to the Axis powers.
3. **The pendulum warning is about timing.** Without formation (structure, roles, love), the swing overshoots into authoritarianism.
4. **Subsidiarity is simultaneously political, theological, and cosmological.** It's a claim about the structure of reality itself.
5. **The meaning crisis is a crisis of symbolic literacy.** We haven't just lost beliefs — we've lost the cognitive/spiritual capacity to experience meaning through participation.
6. **Mary Harrington's "reactionary feminism" is subsidiarity applied to gender.** Remove local structures, get subjection to impersonal forces.
