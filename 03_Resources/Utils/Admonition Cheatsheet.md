# 📌 Admonition Cheatsheet

> [!note] Note
> Use this for general notes or info.

> [!abstract] Abstract
> A short summary or abstract section.

> [!info] Info
> Supplemental information.

> [!tip] Tip
> Helpful advice.

> [!success] Success
> For wins, completed tasks, achievements.

> [!question] Question
> Raise a question to think about later.

> [!warning] Warning
> Highlight potential risks or cautions.

> [!failure] Failure
> Show an error, mistake, or something that didn’t work.

> [!danger] Danger
> Use sparingly — critical warnings.

> [!bug] Bug
> Log issues, errors, or unexpected behaviors.

> [!example] Example
> Illustrate with examples.

> [!quote] Quote
> Highlight quotes or excerpts.
