# Pilot Concept – Deák Húsmíves Online Platform

_Version: 1.3 | Last updated: 2026-03-05_

---

## Origin of the Idea

A local artisan butcher shop operates three locations in Székelyudvarhely (~30,000 residents). They produce additive-free, natural meat products with genuine craft quality. However, the business significantly underperforms due to weak management, lack of systems, and no digital presence.

The owner is an excellent master butcher — but not business or systems oriented. Two sibling co-owners are in conflict with each other.

The opportunity: build a simple online pre-ordering and home delivery system. This would:
- Increase revenue without adding store staff or physical infrastructure
- Reduce fixed costs (in-store sales) while production capacity already exists
- Leverage their existing delivery capability (vehicle + cold chain)
- Differentiate through genuine quality (additive-free, artisan, local)

Strategically, this is not just a local business optimization — it's an **experiment in building a passive income digital product** for Exar Labs, moving away from purely hourly-rate outsourcing toward a scalable, high-value model.

If the model works → validated template for other local businesses → path toward a **Local Artisan Commerce Platform**.

## Exar Labs Strategic Fit

This is not a software project — it is a **co-venture**. Exar Labs acts as a full operational partner, not a vendor.

**Division of responsibilities:**

| Area | Butcher Shop | Exar Labs |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Meat processing & quality | ✓ | — |
| Delivery & cold chain | ✓ | — |
| Online platform (build & maintain) | — | ✓ |
| Marketing (strategy, execution, budget) | — | ✓ |
| Business development | — | ✓ |
| Customer support | — | ✓ |
| Analytics & measurement | — | ✓ |

**The butcher shop's job:** focus entirely on what they do best — producing quality, additive-free meat and delivering it. Nothing else is required from them.

**Exar Labs' investment:**
- Development from own capital (bench capacity + AI tooling)
- Marketing budget from own capital
- Annual operating costs (~3,900 EUR) borne by Exar Labs
- Return: revenue share (X% of online turnover)

**Why this model works for Exar Labs:**
- AI tools reduce development cost to ~1/5 of traditional — makes the upfront investment manageable
- Bench capacity is otherwise idle — cost is already absorbed
- If it works: validated, fully-operational template replicable to other local artisan businesses
- Builds direct client relationship and full go-to-market experience (Pillar 2 & 3 of 24-month roadmap)
- Marketing ownership = Exar Labs controls the growth lever, not dependent on the client's capability

**Estimated annual operating cost (Exar Labs):** ~3,900 EUR (support: 3,000 + hosting: 200 + payment gateway: 200 + customer service: 500) + marketing budget (TBD)

## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Low adoption (people don't order online) | Medium | Pilot validates this fast — limited loss if fails; Exar Labs controls marketing to maximize reach |
| Marketing spend exceeds return | Medium | Set a clear pilot budget cap; measure CAC from day one |
| Payment issues | Low | Cash on delivery in pilot phase |
| Shop closes | Low | Business is stable (just chaotic) |
| Owner conflict blocks production/delivery | Medium | Exar Labs' exposure limited — we control platform & marketing; conflict affects only fulfillment side |
| Ongoing feature requests | Medium | Scope locked for pilot; new features post-validation |
| Butcher shop disengages from the partnership | Low-Medium | Revenue share ensures their incentive; written agreement recommended |

## Pilot Exit Criteria

**Scale decision (continue → invest more):** All 3 of the following met after 30 days:
- ✅ 30+ registrations
- ✅ 15+ orders placed
- ✅ 5+ returning customers

**Pivot decision (rethink model):** 1–2 metrics partially met — analyze blockers, consider adjustments.

**Stop decision (kill pilot):** Fewer than 10 registrations and fewer than 5 orders after 30 days with full marketing effort — insufficient demand signal.

The stop decision is not a failure. It is a fast, cheap answer to a strategic question that protects Exar Labs from further investment in an unvalidated channel.
