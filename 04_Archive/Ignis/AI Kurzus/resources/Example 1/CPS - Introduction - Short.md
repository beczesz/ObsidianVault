# Cloud Platform Services (CPS)

## Short Operational Context (AI Version)

---

# 0. Corporate Context – Sonrisa

CPS operates as a specialized Business Unit within **Sonrisa**, a 19-year independent Central European technology provider with 300+ engineers across Hungary, Romania, and Serbia.

Sonrisa delivers enterprise-grade technology services across six pillars:

- AI Enablement
    
- Cloud Platform Services
    
- System Development
    
- IT Consultancy
    
- Legacy Modernization
    
- Continuity Services
    

Sonrisa is an official AWS partner and has extensive experience with Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, and Oracle Cloud.

CPS is the dedicated cloud and DevOps unit within this broader organization.

---

# 1. CPS Identity & Philosophy

CPS operates as the **“Backstage Crew”** of client organizations.

We:

- Enable stable and secure cloud operations
    
- Operate invisibly in the background
    
- Support — not replace — client product teams
    
- Deliver through structured teams, not individual heroes
    
- Focus on long-term reliability, scalability, and knowledge distribution
    

Our role is foundational, not domain-dominant.

---

# 2. Operational Definition

CPS is a managed cloud and DevOps service unit delivering:

- Scalable fixed-fee support packages
    
- Cloud engineering and modernization projects
    
- Architecture advisory and optimization services
    

The objective is to ensure cost-efficient, reliable, and scalable cloud environments for clients.

---

# 3. Service Model (Support Packages)

CPS operates through structured monthly support tiers:

- **Safety Net** – 6h/month (backup & standby support)
    
- **Essential** – 40h/month
    
- **Growth** – 80h/month
    
- **Scale** – 120h/month
    
- **Custom Enterprise Support** – tailored scope
    

Optional Add-ons:

- 24/7 On-Call Support
    
- Solution Architect Advisory
    
- FinOps Optimization
    
- DevSecOps Enhancements
    
- Quarterly Architecture Reviews
    

Packages provide predictable pricing and scalable support.

---

# 4. Delivery Model

CPS delivers services through structured team units:

- Senior / Lead Engineers
    
- Expert Engineers
    
- Advanced Engineers
    
- Junior Engineers
    

Work is allocated based on task complexity to ensure:

- Cost efficiency
    
- Knowledge distribution
    
- Reduced single-person dependency
    
- Sustainable long-term support
    

Each client engagement is backed by the CPS unit — not a single engineer.

---

# 5. Engagement Types

CPS operates under three primary engagement modes:

1. **Recurring Support Packages**  
    Fixed monthly scope with defined SLA and dedicated hours.
    
2. **Project-Based Engagements (T&M or Fixed Price)**  
    Cloud migrations, modernization, CI/CD implementation, security upgrades.
    
3. **Advisory & Premium Services**  
    Architecture consulting, FinOps optimization, DevSecOps automation.
    

Projects often transition into recurring support relationships.

---

# 6. Core Differentiation

CPS differentiates through:

- Predictable flat-rate pricing (vs. pure hourly consulting)
    
- Team-based delivery model (vs. single DevOps hire)
    
- Proactive monitoring and optimization (vs. reactive cloud provider support)
    
- Backed by a 300+ engineer parent organization (Sonrisa)
    

---

# 7. Positioning Summary

CPS is not:

- A staff augmentation agency
    
- A body-leasing DevOps provider
    
- A cloud reseller
    
- A pure consultancy
    

CPS is a structured cloud operations and DevOps service unit delivering scalable, proactive, and cost-optimized cloud management backed by Sonrisa’s enterprise-scale capabilities.
