## 2. Webinar Introduction (Landing Page Text)

---

**Every week there's a new AI feature. But what about you?**

Most AI webinars tell you what's new. This one tells you what never changes.

As Microsoft Copilot becomes part of everyday work, small business teams face a real question: how do you get the benefits of AI without losing the human qualities that make your business valuable — your judgment, your relationships, your sense of purpose?

In this practical, 75-minute session, two experts will show you exactly how to use Copilot to handle the routine — while sharpening the four things only humans can bring to work.

**What you'll walk away with:**
- A clear picture of what Microsoft Copilot can (and can't) do in your daily Microsoft 365 environment
- A simple framework for keeping humans at the center as AI takes over routine tasks
- Practical tips across 4 dimensions: responsibility, understanding, empathy, and purpose
- Live Copilot demonstrations tailored to small business scenarios
- Confidence to start using AI tomorrow — without a big IT budget or technical background

**Who should attend:**
Small business owners, managers, HR professionals, and team leads who use Microsoft 365 and want to build a human-centered AI practice.

**Format:** Live webinar | 75 minutes including Q&A
**Date:** March 24, 2026 | 10:00 AM
